### Core Purpose

This Skill is specifically designed for male-oriented short dramas (such as War God, comeback, and super-rich themes), aiming to work with video AI tools like Seedance 2.0 to transform textual inspiration into 15-second video generation prompts and director's working scripts with strong visual impact and high emotional density.

### Applicable Scenarios

- **Hit Script Creation**: Quickly build short drama outlines featuring a "golden 3 seconds" and "paywall hooks".
- **Storyboard Prompt Generation**: Automatically calculate dialogue volume and generate 15-second segmented video prompts that meet speech rate standards.
- **Visual Asset Setup**: Provide image-generation instructions for characters and scenes in three visual styles: realistic, CG, and anime.

### Workflow Steps

1. **Confirm Visual Style**: First confirm the style direction (realistic/CG/anime) and aspect ratio (9:16 recommended) with the user.
2. **Plan the Script Outline**: Distill core conflicts and identity reversals, and design hooks for each of the 10 episodes.
3. **Extract Visual Assets**: Based on the style template, generate character three-view drawings, multi-angle scenes, and core prop prompts.
4. **Generate the Director's Script**: Write a single-episode script (300–500 characters) and calculate the required number of video segments.
5. **Output Video Prompts**: For each 15-second segment, generate a video prompt containing "mandatory director instructions".

### Output Requirements

- **Visual Iron Rules**: Cinematic strong light-shadow contrast, with sharp highlights and deep shadows; characters must not face the camera directly (primarily shot from 15–45° side angles).
- **Directing Logic**: Strictly adhere to the 180-degree axis rule; crossing the axis is strictly prohibited; use medium close-ups, close-ups, and shallow depth of field compositions.
- **Dialogue Compliance**: Dialogue speech rate is limited to no more than 4 characters per second (maximum 60 characters per 15 seconds), and the script must be reproduced verbatim—arbitrary additions or deletions are strictly prohibited.
- **Frame Prohibitions**: No subtitles, watermarks, or text/graphics of any kind may appear in the video frames.

### Notes

- Each creation session must confirm the visual style first before proceeding to subsequent steps.
- Image-generation prompts for characters and props must mandatorily use a pure black or dark gray background to ensure consistent visual texture.
