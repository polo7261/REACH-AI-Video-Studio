## Skill Overview

This Skill aims to deeply replicate the cinematic aesthetics and narrative logic of director James Cameron. Through his signature "six-dimensional mental model" and "Visual DNA," it transforms ordinary scripts into epic, high-tech, physically authentic cinematic-grade visual works.

## Core Logic

1. **Narrative Engine**: Focuses on cross-class romance, disaster countdowns, self-sacrifice, and the conflict between "technology and humanity."
2. **Visual DNA**:
   - **Color Formula**: A high-contrast complementary palette of cold steel blue (#4A6B8A) and flame orange (#D4782F).
   - **Camera Language**: Steadicam long takes, POV perspectives, low-angle shots looking up at mechanical giants, emphasizing the continuity of physical logic.
   - **Audiovisual Philosophy**: Liquid/fluid visual motifs, along with sound design logic that emphasizes the sense of mechanical mass.

## Applicable Inputs

- Film or short drama script fragments
- Story synopses or worldbuilding concepts
- Specific visual creation requirements

## Operating Steps

1. **Style Conversion**: Reconstruct the input content based on Cameron's decision heuristics (e.g., "the hero must endure," "the villain is technologized").
2. **Asset Planning**:
   - **Character Design**: Generate professional-grade character three-view drawings, detail breakdowns, and multi-expression close-ups.
   - **Scene Design**: Adopt film-grade nine-grid layouts covering wide shots, bird's-eye views, and material details.
   - **Prop Design**: Output realistic product three-view drawings.
3. **Video Breakdown**: Automatically segment the script at 2.5 characters/second, calculating the action beats and durations for each segment.
4. **Prompt Generation**: Inject Cameron-style prefixes (side view, shallow depth of field, blue-orange backlighting) to generate video prompts compatible with Seedance 2.0.

## Output Requirements

- **Shooting Plan**: Complete character design sheets, scene nine-grids, and prop drawings.
- **Video Segmentation Table**: Includes the start and end times of each video segment, dialogue content, action descriptions, and visual style parameters.
- **Technical Parameters**: Strictly control depth of field, composition angles, and color logic to ensure the visuals carry the rhythm of either a "Cameron-style explosion" or "Cameron-style stillness."

## Notes

- Mockumentary shaky-cam style is prohibited; spatial relationships must remain absolutely clear.
- The protagonist must navigate through collapsing physical systems and bear the cost, rejecting a mediocre logic of victory.
- The ending must carry a meaningful legacy, avoiding nihilistic tendencies.
