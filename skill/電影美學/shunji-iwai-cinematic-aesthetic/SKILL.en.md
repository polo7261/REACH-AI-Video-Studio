# Shunji Iwai Film Aesthetics

> 🔴 **Please strictly follow this SKILL step by step.** Version: v4.1.6

## Underlying DNA (Unconditional, Applied to Every Single Shot)

1. 🔴 **Backlight as the absolute key light (Iwai's signature, most important)**: Light floods in massively from behind or side-behind the subject, or through a window; backlight serves as the frame's main light source rather than fill light — highlights spill in large soft halos from the subject's contours, hair tips, and shoulder lines, blooming into overexposure; hair edges are lit through into translucent bright-gold/bright-white rim light; faces may sit in a soft, light-transmitting half-silhouette. **Bright but never blown to pure white**: highlights glow softly yet retain a trace of layering and color, never washing out into pure white and losing detail. Brighten the whole frame, push it toward the light, **never darken, never low-key**.
2. 🔴 **High-key translucency + halation glow**: The overall image is high-key, bright, translucent, with an airy quality and light haze, as if veiled in a layer of soft light. Highlights soften, bleed, and bloom (halation/bloom); light spots, flares, and floating dust in the air drift within the light. Soft-focus filter texture; eliminate all digital harshness and sharpness, HD documentary feel.
3. **35mm grain + 2000s DV digital-film texture**: Organic film grain + the crisp translucency of early-2000s DV/early digital, with full-bodied rich color carrying film density, and an image that feels of the present moment. Iwai is not a "faded old photograph" — it's digital clarity and sharpness + the rich color and grain of fine film stock; colors are alive, real, present-day, never aged/faded/distressed with vintage filters. What gets removed is only the "harshness and golden-yellow," not color saturation.
4. **Golden-yellow iron-clad ban**: No overall yellowing/golden-orange/amber warm tones whatsoever. Backlit sunsets are always cold white/silver-white/pale-cyan light; highlight halos are cold white, never golden. Even at dusk or under warm indoor lamps, treat with cool tones or neutral-to-cool; warm colors appear only as small accents (a red scarf, slightly warm skin tones), never spread across the frame.
5. **Low contrast + shadows never dead black**: Overall low contrast and soft; shadows are warm brown/soft gray, never pure black or crushed black.
6. **Shallow depth of field soft focus**: Backgrounds melt into soft bokeh; shallow focus with a breathing quality.
7. 🔴 **Emotional core: a bright surface wrapping sadness (Iwai's soul, must be firmly held)**: This is Iwai's most essential temperament — the brighter, more translucent, more brilliantly backlit the image, the more a faint sorrow, wistfulness, unattainable longing, and nostalgia hide beneath. It is not simple "radiant youth"; it is **an unspeakable ache and tenderness beneath the radiant surface**. The frame should have body warmth, a sense of memory, like a stretch of old time that feels "déjà vu, yet impossible to return to." Characters are the absolute protagonists at intimate scale (close-up/medium close-up; the person is the frame's emotional center), not a tiny dot in a vast space. ⚠️ Never let it become cold documentary, and never let it become carefree saccharine xiaoqingxin / influencer-style Japanese aesthetic — the difference lies entirely in this layer of "sadness beneath the brightness."
8. **Wind lets the frame breathe**: Drifting hair / swaying grass / flying petals / snow-lit dust; time is flowing, the air is moving.
9. **Scope**: Only the pure-love fresh aesthetic; not the dark, gritty urban style of Swallowtail Butterfly.

## Texture Suffix (Included by Default in Every Image Prompt)

`35mm film + 2000s DV digital texture, full-bodied rich color with film density, cool neutral tones (no fading, graying, or aging), strong backlight, halation glow blooming within cold white-silver light, high-key bright soft halo that retains layering (never washing to pure white), light transmitting through hair edges, soft-focus filter, high-key translucent brightness, fine film grain, soft hazy focus, low contrast with shadows never dead black, shallow-focus bokeh, the warmth of déjà-vu memories of youth; no yellowing/amber/warm-yellow filters, no aged washed-out look, no fully overexposed dead-white highlights, no dead-black shadows`

## Workflow (8-Step Standard Process)

> 🔴 **Global inquiry spec**: For every step requiring user confirmation/selection, always pop up an **option card**; never degrade into plain-text Q&A. Closed questions get preset options + a custom option; open content (e.g., story synopsis) is carried by two choices: "I'll provide it / Generate for me."

1. **Unified entry inquiry (pop option card)**: One panel asks six items clearly: ① Duration (single shot ≤15s / single episode 30–60s / multi-episode several minutes / custom) ② Execution mode (fully automatic / manual) ③ Aspect ratio (default 4:3, options 16:9/21:9/custom; resolution default 720p) ④ Dialogue language ⑤ 3 core elements (living space / family relationships / everyday objects) ⑥ Whether there is a story outline. After answers, write to memory. Moodboard and story generation actions are deferred to steps 2 and 3.
2. **Style confirmation (generation action)**: Generate a moodboard from the 3 elements (image generation capability, 4:3, a single fused image filling full-bleed, no white borders/collage/paneling) → save as style-anchor asset and record the archive marker. ⚠️ The moodboard must present a real cinematic film texture, **manga feel/illustration feel/anime feel/CG-render feel strictly banned** (no outlined contours, no cel-style/impasto painting style, no overly perfect uniform CG lighting effects).
3. **Story (generation + confirmation bundled with moodboard via option card; skip confirmation in fully automatic mode)**: If there is an outline, follow it and only layer on Iwai stylization; if none, generate from the 3 elements (youth/first love/memory/faint sorrow) → **bundle moodboard and story for unified option-card confirmation** (all confirmed as correct / adjust moodboard / adjust story) → after approval, proceed to step 4.
4. **Assets + storyboard (pop option card; fully automatic mode skips this confirmation)**: First produce assets — every character with lines must get a white-background studio model-card final-look character image (locking face/costume/hairstyle; thereafter always reference via @CharacterName); scene images are single empty shots, never split-screen, and 🎨 **mandatory attachment of the moodboard style anchor as reference** (anchoring only tone/light feel/texture, not composition); named props are summarized in one white-background prop composite image → then, based on the story and assets, produce the **complete storyboard** (each shot: shot size + @character action + camera movement + environment + duration) → assets and storyboard are **bundled for unified confirmation** (confirmed as correct / needs adjustment); only after approval proceed to step 5.
5. **Generate video**: Generate shot by shot per the confirmed storyboard, attaching scene images/character images/prop images to lock consistency, 🔴 **do not attach the moodboard** (this ban applies only to video generation; at step 4's scene-image generation the moodboard style anchor must be attached — do not confuse the two).
6. **Music (generation action, not yet composited)**: After the video is finalized, the agent **automatically selects the style branch based on the script's emotion** per the〈Music〉branch-selection logic and generates directly (default: mureka), without asking the user. **After generation, do not composite yet; hold in staging**, pending the user's decision at step 8.
7. **Composite**: Only when exceeding 15 seconds split into multiple segments, generate serially, then stitch together into the complete final film (no BGM at this point).
8. **Unified post-film processing and inquiry (pop option card)**: One panel, multiple choice: ① Add subtitles ② Composite background music ③ Modify music then composite. Key constraints:
   - 🔴 **Subtitle copy hard rule (inlined in the inquiry items, to prevent mistakenly jumping to monolingual)**: Chinese dialogue → Chinese monolingual subtitles; **non-Chinese** dialogue (Japanese/English etc.) → **bilingual subtitles: top track Chinese, bottom track the film's language** (e.g., Japanese dialogue → Chinese-Japanese bilingual [Chinese on top, Japanese below]; English dialogue → Chinese-English bilingual [Chinese on top, English below]); **non-Chinese must never appear as monolingual original text only**. The subtitle option copy in the option card must state this explicitly per this rule (e.g., "Add Chinese-Japanese bilingual subtitles" rather than "Add subtitles"); before execution, read back and verify once whether the subtitle language configuration matches the dialogue language; if mismatched, correct before executing.
   - **Execution order mandates subtitles first, music after** (the current tool has issues generating subtitles when music exists).
   - **Audio mixing defaults: original sound +8dB, BGM -2dB**.
   - Attached downstream post-production editing tool (agent.tool.edit_video):
     - **Subtitles** add_subtitle: white text with black outline; Chinese dialogue monolingual / non-Chinese bilingual (top track Chinese, bottom track the film's language).
     - **Transitions** transition: default hard cut; if the user requests a transition without specifying the type → fade in/out; options: fade in-out / black frame / white frame / noise dissolve; AI transitions are not in this list. ⚠️ The dissolve/crossfade ban only constrains the video-generation phase; after compositing, transitions can be added normally.
     - **Frame extraction** extract_frames: the agent calls it automatically to help judge transitions; the user may also request it proactively.
- **Cropping** clip: second-level precision, executed after the user describes the content to be cropped.
     - **Text overlay** text_overlay: film title / chapter title card, cast list, name captions; users can customize text content and style.

## Aspect Ratio & Quality (Hardcoded)

moodboard/scene images 4:3; character model cards 16:9; video defaults to 4:3, **720p** (changeable). Images default to the libimage model, 2K resolution, standard quality (user-changeable).

## Image Prompt Language

Mainly Chinese; technical terms (halation/35mm/DV) stay in English when necessary. Negative terms are physically separated from the body and placed in a standalone negative field.

## Character Iron Rules

- **Lock the texture, free the identity**: the 35mm+DV clear cool-toned texture is a hard constraint; hair color/hairstyle/clothing/subculture styling respect the user's settings, no Iwai-fication correction.
- **Skin is the warm exception within cool tones**: overall cool tone, but skin stays translucent, rosy, with a healthy flush—never greenish-gray or dull. Female skin gets an extra notch up (fine, translucent, softly lustrous).
- **Model card ≠ final shots**: model cards use a pure white base + bright, even studio front lighting (face fully brightened, facial details prioritized), never backlight/overexposure. 16:9 layout: left three views (front/side/back) + right large facial close-up (mandatory, cannot be omitted, cannot be drawn as a full body).
- 🔴 **Model card template (fill as-is; do not alter the structure or the trailing texture words, only replace the \[\] placeholders; if the layout breaks apart / views are incomplete, fall back to the English version)**:


## Scene Cards (pull per scene, shared underlying DNA)

- **Backlit close-up**: sun shooting straight into the lens from behind, strong backlight with soft blooming halo, light shining through hair strands, soft-focus glow, cool-toned clarity. Can be half-silhouette (face visible, shadows not crushed black) or pure silhouette.
- **Snow scene (Love Letter)**: cold blue-white high-key snow world + a touch of warm breathing color (orange sweater / red mailbox), soft diffused light (not backlit bloom), falling snow, extremely low contrast.
- **Classroom (Lily Chou-Chou)**: windows as the only light source, strong backlight flooding in to create a sense of light (hazy diffusion, organic and irregular, **absolutely not CG-render-perfect conical volumetric light beams**), damp hazy misting, window-side overexposure coexisting with soft indoor dimness, characters framed with lonely negative space. ⚠️ Three hard constraints: ① **light beams must be organic, irregular, and grained**—no designer volumetric light / particle-effect-style uniform light cones; ② **floating dust must be randomly and unevenly distributed**—no circular uniform particle effects; ③ **character backlit contours must blur naturally**—no hard outlined rim light (the source of a comic look); keep a little soft fill light on the face side so features stay discernible, no pure outline silhouette.
- **Daily outdoor scenes**: streets/riverbanks/routes to school. Sunny-day soft sun, low-angle looking up at blue sky and cherry blossoms (Hana & Alice) / overcast diffusion / dusk cool twilight backlight (gold-orange forbidden).
- 🌾 **Lily Chou-Chou wheat field (signature, must deliver high quality)**: highly saturated rich emerald rice paddy filling the frame (natural pure emerald green, not pink-green/gray-green/warm grass green/blue-leaning), rice stalks and ears clearly visible rather than flat green blocks, daytime strong backlight with a very bright sky that retains layers (not dead white), a boy in a white shirt with headphones, head down, immersed, low camera position with rice waves filling the bottom two-thirds, wind rippling the rice in one shared direction. Negative locks: washed-out whitish green, gray-green, pink mint green, warm yellow grass green, flat smeared blur, invisible rice ears, overall blue-cyan film cast. Whenever the user mentions the wheat field, apply this card unconditionally; after generating, self-check that the green is rich and pure, the rice ears visible, the sky not dead white.
  - Full prompt (ready to use): `Lily Chou-Chou-style signature green rice paddy, highly saturated rich emerald / imperial-green rice field with film density filling the entire frame (natural vivid deep emerald green—absolutely not fresh pink-green, absolutely not washed-out yellow-green, absolutely not warm dry grass green, and no overall blue-cyan cold cast), individual rice stalks and plump drooping rice ears clearly visible, near-field rice leaves sharp with dewdrop reflections, layering into blur toward the distance for depth, daytime strong backlight, sky very bright but still retaining layers and sky color (not fully blown to pure white, highlights softly blooming with detail preserved), low camera position or chest-level view, surging rice waves filling the bottom two-thirds of the frame with a strip of bright yet layered sky overhead, wind blowing the whole paddy into a single surging wave rippling in one direction, soft focus and glow, early-2000s DV texture with dense color (not faded, not aged), 35mm-like grain, shadows as warm black, not dead black`

## Camera Moves (one per shot, meshed with the action, dynamic by default)

Fast lateral tracking / handheld impact follow / backlit pans / abrupt push-pull / locked low-angle / jump-cut position leaps. ⚙️ Ultra-slow push-in and high-frame-rate slow motion are disabled by default, enabled only when the user names them.

## Editing Pacing (one dynamic fast-cut piece by default)

- Real-time speed and hard cuts between shots by default; no lyrical long takes, no high-frame-rate slow motion, no dissolve transitions (enabled only when the user names them).
- Shot size may jump at most one step between adjacent shots; no violent jumps (close-up ↔ extreme wide).
- The same action across cuts must change in discrete states (running → stopping → glancing back → turning around), one new action beat per shot.

## Music (pure instrumental, no lyrics, mureka by default)

- Temperament: restrained, spacious, crystalline, never sentimental—driven by tone color and atmosphere rather than beats (the Hot-Blooded Youth track excepted).
- 🎹 **Track 1 · Pure-Love Freshness** (piano-led + string support) three sub-tracks:
  - A · Nostalgia/memories: spatial single piano notes, warm string bed, clear melody. For flashbacks / narration / end credits.
  - B · First love/giddiness: brightened-up piano + pizzicato/bell accents, light and springy with a sense of anticipation. For first encounters / before confessions / easy daily life.
  - C · Release/parting: long string tones with a strong sense of breath, melody sparse or even absent, open and weightless. For farewells / turning away / figures walking into the distance.
- 🌫️ **Track 2 · Aching Ethereal**: no instrument limits—Debussy-style impressionist piano / ambient noise / synth pads / ethereal wordless textures, weak melody, strong atmosphere. For loneliness / alienation / alone in the wheat field / the ache of youth.
- ⚡ **Track 3 · Hot-Blooded Youth**: string-driven (rhythmic bowed-string propulsion) + light percussion accents (not heavy drums) + bright with a sense of speed. The only track permitted rhythmic propulsion, yet still restrained—the rush of "riding a bike straight into the backlight," not a competitive burst. For cycling and running / school festival / sports meet / collective momentum scenes.
- **Track selection**: memories/voiceover→A; meeting/confession→B; farewell/letting go→C; loneliness/alienation→second track; running/driving momentum→third track. Multiple tracks coexist within the same video and switch with each section.
- No heavy drumbeats/pop arrangements/strong beat-syncing/orchestral explosions/electronic dance music/vocals with lyrics. The rhythm of the hot-blooded youth track = the sense of speed of bowed strings, not percussion explosions.

## Post-Generation Self-Check (redo if standards are not met)

Are the shadows crushed black/oppressive (forbidden)? Is there an overall golden-orange cast (forbidden; must be a cool silver-white tone)? Are the backlight and hair translucency present? Is it bright and transparent enough? Are highlights softly halated with gradation preserved rather than overexposed dead white? Are colors saturated with a present-day feel rather than dull, grayish, and artificially aged? Are characters solid and clear, not dissolved or vanished? Are transitions hard cuts without dissolves? In the wheat field shot, is the green rich and pure, are the rice ears present, is the sky not dead white?

## Video Iron Rules

- 🔴 **Multi-shot within a single video (mandatory by default)**: seedance runs at most 15 seconds per generation and natively supports multiple shots within a single video plus hard cuts between shots; **do not use "one shot per video + post-editing splicing"**. Self-assess total duration from story beats; do not force it to fill 15 seconds: first estimate the number of action beats (one beat = one complete action state that can stand alone as a shot) to determine shot count and duration (2 beats→8s, 2 shots / 3→10s, 3 shots / 4→12s, 4 shots / 5→15s, 5 shots). **Only split into multiple videos when exceeding 15 seconds, then splice them serially using the video compositing capability**. The prompt advances second by second; each shot = shot size + @character action + camera movement + environment; hard cuts between shots explicitly break the segments.
- **No BGM output**: video generation needs only ambient sound + dialogue; BGM is overlaid at the composition stage. The prompt permanently declares "keep only ambient sound and dialogue, no background music, no score"; add "background music, score" to the negative prompt. BGM is generated per workflow step 6 after the video is finalized and staged; it is composited into the final video only at step 8 after user confirmation, and **must be executed after subtitle processing is complete** (compositing subtitles and music at the same time causes problems); mixing defaults to original sound +8dB, BGM -2dB.
- 🔴 **In group/multi-person scenes, everyone must be moving**: when two or more people appear in the frame, **every character must have a visible action or state** (walking, talking, turning, subtle movement, etc.); no character may stand frozen while only the protagonist moves—unless the user explicitly requires a character to remain still or stand in place. Each character's own action must be written into the prompt; add "background characters standing stiff, frozen like wax figures" to the negative prompt.
- **Narration strategy**: narration is uniformly **written into the video prompt and generated by the video model along with the visuals** (no separate voice recording, no post overlay). Shots requiring narration go into the timeline marked "Narration (voiceover): …"; narration text is bound by the language lock (use the selected dialogue language); narration wording is restrained and plain—prefer less over more—matching Iwai's negative-space sensibility.
- **Three-pronged skin tone treatment for video (video prompt only; static images/model cards untouched)**: to counter video models degrading real skin texture into a dirty look—① slightly lift shadows by about one stop (a localized de-dulling lift, without pushing the overall image to high-key); ② purify skin tone, remove color noise and color casts; ③ restrain excessive sharpening and oily sheen, but skin smoothing remains forbidden.
- 🔴 **Language lock**: every video prompt permanently declares "all character dialogue/narration in \[selected language\]; even if the model improvises lines it must use only this language; dialogue/subtitles/lip-sync in other languages forbidden" (write it whether or not the shot has dialogue); add "dialogue in other languages, mixed-language lip-sync" to the negative prompt. Prevents the wrong language from popping up when the model spontaneously adds lines to dialogue-free shots.
- **Character solidity**: characters remain solid and clear throughout, never dissolving/semi-transparent/vanishing/swallowed by light or environment. "Backlight translucency/surrounded/walking into depth of field" is static atmosphere; in video it must never be executed as characters disappearing. Permanent negatives: character dissolving, semi-transparent, vanishing, merging into background.
- **No dissolves/cross-fades**: hard cuts between shots; permanent negatives: no dissolve/cross-fade/fade.
