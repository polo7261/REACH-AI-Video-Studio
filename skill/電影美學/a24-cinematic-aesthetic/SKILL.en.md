# A24 Film Aesthetics

> 🔴 Follow this SKILL strictly, step by step. Version 2.5.1 lean (adapted for Seedance 2.5)

## Underlying DNA (applied unconditionally to every visual)

1. 🔴 **Authentic film texture as the absolute base**: live-action photographic grade, 16mm/35mm grain, skin with visible pores and a natural oil sheen. No CGI/anime/3D/plastic skin/excessive skin smoothing/harsh digital sharpness. 🔴 Higher resolution does not mean cleaner—720p/1080p must likewise retain visible grain, halation, and a low-contrast curve.
2. 🔴 **Emotion takes priority over plot**: the frame serves inner emotion, not information delivery. The overall rhythm is fast and taut, oppressive, offering no room to breathe; but the tension comes from emotional pressure, not fancy editing.
3. **Intimacy + unease coexisting**: beauty and discomfort in the same frame, shallow depth of field up close, direct facial gazes, the uncanny within the everyday.
4. **Restrained composition; negative space means tension**: negative space and silence are stored energy (the hush before the storm), not slackness; symmetrical geometry without showing off.
5. **Motivated light sources**: natural light/practical sources (windows/lamps/candles/neon/fluorescent); the effect of light is seen, never the fixture.
6. **The medium must move**: fog/dust/rain/snow/light dust with direction + speed stated explicitly; clothing and hair take the wind in sync.
7. 🔴 **Temperament base + reference library**: references serve only as directional anchors (light color/texture/identifiable signatures); the output is always original, never a shot-by-shot replica.
8. **Palette follows emotion**: bold saturation or calm naturalism are both allowed; the sole negative lock: avoid the default faded-yellow cheap look.
9. Only the A24 auteurist film lineage; no commercial fast-cut showmanship, no pure genre-film visuals.

## Texture tail words (included by default with every image prompt)

`Shot on 16mm or 35mm film, natural film grain, low-contrast film curve, realistic skin rendering (real pores and texture), soft halation, authentic gritty texture, emotional cinematic color grade, intimate shallow depth of field, motivated natural/practical lighting, no over-sharpening, no CG-rendered feel; not anime, not CGI, not a 3D render, not plastic skin, not over-smoothed, not harsh digital HD sharpness, no default faded-yellow tone, no high-resolution digital cleanliness (retain the grain and noise structure), no studio lighting fixtures in frame, no black letterbox bars (the image fills the entire frame, full bleed)`

10. **Post-chain limitations**: available: subtitles/transitions (default hard cut)/frame extraction/cropping/text overlays/mixing; **no speed changes, no denoising, no super-resolution**—all camera movement must be accomplished within the prompt; to change it, go through step 6. Voice is burned in with the video, not added in post. Editing temperament: fast and tight, pressing forward one beat after another, never releasing pressure.

## Video Iron Rules

- 🔴 **Shot segmentation = content-event driven**: cut to a new shot as soon as any one of these appears: ① action-beat change ② emotional turning point ③ need to change shot size or perspective ④ shift in attention focus. Follow the content—don't hard-cut just to hit a count, and don't skip cuts to save effort; count and duration are **outcomes** (with MAXDUR=30: 5–8 shots, 2–5 seconds each); tension favors more shots, slow dark current favors longer shots. 🔴 See the MAXDUR table for lower limits; a true one-take must explicitly state "single shot, no cut" in the prompt and give the reason. The timeline uses continuous absolute time across the whole film, with each segment labeled by its cut motivation.
- 🔴 **Four tracks mandatory**, every timeline segment must include:
  1. **Subject action**: inherited from the story's Action (never improvised) = action + motivation + process breakdown ("stop and turn" → footsteps slowing to a halt/shoulders turn first, head follows/gaze locks in).
  2. 🔴 **Emotion externalization**: face (brows-eyes/mouth corners/breathing) + micro-expression pauses (hesitation/gaze drifting off then returning) + body (shoulder-neck tension/finger micro-movements/weight shift). 🎭 Use the signature micro-gestures from the character performance keynote. ⚠️ Be specific enough to act (not "tense expression" but "Adam's apple bobbing, knuckles pressed white against the table edge"); include layered turns (escalation/restraint/breaking point); pauses are taut, not relaxed. 💡 2.5 acting is restrained and micro-expressions are readable—write the subtle layers; no need for wide eyes, furrowed brows, or an open mouth to "make sure it shows".
  3. **Camera movement**: pick 1 from the camera-movement library; write type + speed + direction.
  4. **Medium/environment dynamics**: wind/light dust/rain in continuous motion.
- **Character physicality**: entities stay clear throughout; no dissolving/translucency/vanishing/being swallowed by light or environment.
- 🔴 **Style source = scene images (plus prop and character images); attaching a moodboard is forbidden**: Scene images have already absorbed the moodboard at the image layer; a video that faithfully references the scene images inherits their style. Shots share the same set of references so the temperament doesn't drift. Before dispatching a task, check the reference list item by item to confirm there is no moodboard.
- 🔴 **Reference discipline**: Hard cap of 30 images/10 audio clips (🔴 video references not used), **practically 3–8 items** (1 scene image + split-screen images of appearing characters + key props + (optional) voice audio). Every item must state its purpose; no purpose, no attachment. 🔴 **Never attach @video references** (neither as a continuation source nor as a camera-movement reference); the video stage consumes only images and optional voice audio.
- 🔴 **Absolutely no BGM**: Output only ambient sound + dialogue (+ narration); score/SFX underscore is forbidden and music-type @audio references are forbidden; the negative prompt must include "background music, film score".
- 🎙️ **Dual voice tracks (③/④)**: dialogue = lip-synced track; narration = off-screen track without lip-sync; in ④, assign each track its own time slot on the timeline (narration fills the gaps between dialogue, never sounding simultaneously), spelled out in one pass within the same prompt; the voice must stay consistent across segments.
- 🔴 **Language lock**: Every prompt permanently carries "dialogue/narration always in the selected language; any lines the model adds must also use only this language; dialogue/subtitles/lip-sync in other languages are forbidden". ⚠️ Only locks the spoken language inside the film; it does not change the language the prompt itself is written in.
- 🔴 **Don't flatten**: Doubling the duration means raising the number of storyboard shots, not making each shot longer; check for "idle stretches stretched out just to fill the runtime". The same action across shots must change state in discrete steps; leave no idle time between beats.

## Screenplay

- 🔴 **Default rhythm = escalating tense pressure**: The very first scene carries unresolved tension, escalating scene by scene without venting; scenes press forward in tight succession, information restrained; the ending stays open with no answers. Tension comes from undercurrents of "the situation feels off / relationships breaking down / something is coming", not plot twists or mystery puzzles.
- 🎬 **Optional slow undercurrent** (in the vein of The Banshees of Inisherin / Aftersun / Lady Bird): Long breathing, negative space, stacked everyday details, tension seeping out beneath the calm; "fast and tight" yields, while still keeping emotion first + open ending + refusing to preach. 🎥 This paradigm prefers MAXDUR long takes for a "single-take gaze" (slow push-in / slow drift / subtle orbit, no cuts).
- ⚠️ **One rhythm per film**: Decide "tense pressure" or "slow undercurrent" at the outset, unified across the whole film (local abrupt halts/bursts of contrast are allowed; the main keynote is singular).
- Rhythm aligns with the emotional arc and carries through to cutting and camera movement; it only affects event density, not the "cut by content events" rule.
- 💬 **Dialogue (②/④)**: 🔴 Whenever two or more characters share a scene or have a motive to interact, write actual lines — don't write characters as mutes; dialogue volume is set per scene, with the speaker labeled for every line. 🎭 Lifelike, colloquial, brief; 🔴 **leave subtext** (answering off the question, leaving sentences half-said, wrapping inner troubles in trivia); **anti-aphorism**: no lines that summarize the theme or explain emotions ("I'm sad" is forbidden); use concrete words or silence instead.
- 🎙️ **Narration (③/④)**: Restrained and intimate, as if written to oneself; not omniscient commentary, no restating the picture. 🔴 The text is finalized at the screenplay stage and burned into the video; nothing is left for post.
- 🔴 **Segment writing**: One task carries "one complete small emotional rise and fall" (setup → build → turn → unresolved close), not forcibly stretched short shots; line density is matched to the actual duration, and pauses and silences occupy duration as well.

## Character Iron Rules

- **Lock the texture, free the identity**: Film texture is a hard constraint; hair color/hairstyle/clothing/subcultural styling respect the user's settings.
- **Race defaults** to Western or Asian-Western; user specification takes priority.
- 🔴 **Gender must be assigned (including robots/AI/humanoids)**: Every character with scenes gets a definite gender (male/female); lock the visual lean and the voice timbre together, consistent across the film with no voice change, and write it into the character asset.
- **Realistic skin**: Pores/texture/natural sheen; plastic faces and digital over-sharpness are forbidden (doubly emphasized at high resolution).
- **Character asset = a single left-right split-screen image**: Left half a full-body three-view (front/side/back), right half facial details; pure white background, studio lighting, neutral pose, no props.
- 🔴 **Character images carry no props** (close-fitting clothing doesn't count); 🔴 **Child safety**: For young/school-age characters, combining specific age numbers, body measurement descriptions, and adultified clothing is forbidden; always use school-age temperament words.
- 🔴 **Split-screen template (fill in as-is; only replace the \[\] placeholders; changing the structure or the trailing texture words is forbidden)**:

```

Left half: full-body three-view of the same character, presenting full-body standing poses from the front, side, and back views side by side, front view clearly facing forward, both feet in footwear visible (shoes by default, shoe style matching the outfit), neutral relaxed pose, used to lock physique, clothing, and hairstyle.
