## Three Visual-Tone Presets (Choose One at Stage 1, Unified Across the Whole Film)

All three **share one fixed seed**; only the color grade / light quality / emotional language changes. The clauses below are used as-is: **the "color clause" goes into the MJ scene-image prompt, and the "leading style sentence" is placed as the first sentence of the Seedance prompt.**

### A · Cold-Tone Sculptural

Primarily a cold cinematic grade (steel blue / cool cyan, desaturated, deep contrast); hard single light, cathedral-style light beams, black negative space; minimalist brutalism + religious-feeling sacred spaces / vast empty megastructures; solemn and stately. **Black-and-white is an optional sub-mode.**

- Color clause (cold tone): `cold-tone desaturated cinematic grade, steel-blue to cool-cyan palette, deep contrast with crushed shadows, a single hard sculptural key light, a vast solemn brutalist or sacred space, solemn silence, fine 35mm film grain`
- Color clause (optional B&W, replaces the clause above): `high-contrast black and white, deep crushed blacks, a single hard key light`
- Leading style sentence (cold tone): `cold-tone desaturated cinematic MV footage, steel-blue to cool-cyan palette, deep contrast and a hard sculptural key light, a vast solemn brutalist or sacred space, a solemn silent atmosphere, fine 35mm film grain, natural handheld with a slight breathing feel.`
- Leading style sentence (B&W): `high-contrast black-and-white MV footage, deep crushed blacks and a single hard key light, a solemn grand space, fine 35mm film grain, natural handheld with a slight breathing feel.`

### B · Warm-Tone Film

Warm 35mm, golden light, natural skin tones, rich midtones, grain; color and B&W may be mixed; hometown-neighborhood realism + theatrical symbols; best at 4:3, works for choreography / crowds.

- Color clause: `warm 35mm cinematic film grade, golden natural light, rich midtones, authentic neighborhood-block realism, heavy film grain, subtle halation`
- Leading style sentence: `warm 35mm cinematic MV footage, golden natural light and rich midtones, grounded neighborhood realism with a touch of theatrical staging, heavy film grain, natural handheld with organic movement.`

### C · Sharp-Flash Grain

Acid-green / lime color cast or cold-blue club tone, lo-fi, heavy grain, sweaty highlights; hard on-camera flash + club strobe / neon; playful, provocative candids.

- Color clause: `lo-fi grainy party texture, acid lime-green color cast, hard on-camera flash light, club-style high contrast, sweaty highlights, heavy grain`
- Leading style sentence: `lo-fi grainy party MV footage, acid lime-green color cast and hard on-camera flash light, club-style high contrast with a sweaty feel, heavy grain, relaxed candid handheld with crisp movement.`

## Fixed Seed & Image-Output Specs (Quick Reference)

> **Global defaults**: **MJ platform image quality "Adaptive", Lib Image "Standard quality"**; **each MJ prompt ≤ 1024 characters** (trailing strings/parameters included; if over, trim the description instead of piling on words). Use **V8.2** uniformly wherever MJ is used. **All prompts are in Chinese (parameters/seed codes are not translated); use positive, compliant words for clothing, and no sensitive words in the negative section.**

- **Styling image = Seedream 5.0 Pro** (**2K, with no trailing strings/parameters of any kind**): produce **one 16:9 styling image on a white background, with 4 full-body looks side by side from left to right, the 4 looks clearly distinct from one another**; real-life model, face and full body clearly visible; the user **picks 1** of these 4 (the chosen look is locked in as the whole film's main look). **If a styling reference image exists, it serves as Seedream's image reference; portrait/face reference images are never fed to Seedream.** The prompt is pure Chinese natural language (gender/ethnicity/styling direction + clothing/character baseline requirements), **with no** `--sref` **/** `--ar` **/** `--v` **/** `--no` **or any other trailing strings**.\
  Example: `A 16:9, 2K high-definition styling reference image on a white background; the same [gender][ethnicity] real-life model, 4 full-body looks side by side from left to right, the 4 looks clearly distinct from one another; face and full body clearly visible, natural hands, tasteful modest fashion, private parts covered, non-see-through fabric, non-erotic, clean skin with no tattoos and no piercings; pure white seamless background, even studio lighting, photographic realism`
- **Three-view image = Lib Image** (mandatory, default **Standard quality**); once the styling look is confirmed, **expand the chosen full-body look into a white-background full-body three-view image**; **you must connect the upstream [chosen styling image] node to Lib Image's image reference input — a hard requirement; without the connection, Lib Image will not follow the selected look and will drift into a different one**. **Real-life model**; **layout is mandatory: all content in a single image, 16:9, full body in left/center/right three views (front/side/back), no head close-ups / big-head expression three views**; **with a portrait reference image → the faces in the three views are fully covered by a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese text in the bottom-right corner "Face provided separately · This image is for clothing-styling reference only"** (the real face is locked from the original image in Seedance); **without a portrait reference image → keep the face from the chosen Seedream styling look** (the face is the identity).\
  Key points: `A single three-view character sheet expanded from the selected full-body look, 16:9, the same real-life photographic model's full body in left/center/right (front/side/back) three views, with the side and back consistently inferred from the selected look, full-body three views only, no head close-up three views, styling and body proportions exactly consistent across all views; clean skin with no tattoos or piercings, tasteful modest clothing, private parts covered, non-see-through fabric, non-erotic, pure white seamless background, even soft studio lighting, photographic realism, (only when an original face reference exists: in each view the face's features are fully covered by a pure white solid circular sticker, and one line of small Chinese text is placed in the image's bottom-right corner "Face provided separately · This image is for clothing-styling reference only")`
- **Scene image = Midjourney V8.2** (default **Adaptive**, prompt ≤ 1024 characters), fixed seed `--sref 5822120099`, **panoramic wide shot, no people, zero text**.\
  Trailing string: `… --sref 5822120099 --ar <16:9|4:3|9:16> --v 8.2 --no text, letters, numbers, subtitles, signboard text, logo, watermark, cartoon, illustration`
- **Video = Seedance 2.5**; inputs = scene image + white-background three-view image (locks the look/physique) + **face reference** (with a portrait reference image → the original image/video; without one → the face from the chosen Seedream styling look; **portrait reference images are never fed to the styling model, only fed here**).

## Workflow (Pause for Confirmation at Each Stage)

### Stage 0 · Before the Shoot

**Pre-shoot questionnaire card (each question below is a standalone question, asked one after another within the same card; never blend two questions' content into one question's options, and never split them across multiple cards. Once the questions are done, stop and wait for the upload)**:

> Rule: **each question asks exactly one thing and offers only clean options for that question**; **conditional questions (Q2 style & theme, Q5 ethnicity) are always displayed as usual — when not applicable, simply let the user "leave blank / skip"; never merge them into another question**.

1. **Audio (single choice; give only these two options, don't stuff anything else in)**: Do you have an existing song / BGM?
   - Option A: `I have existing audio, will upload it later`
   - Option B: `No, generate one for me`
2. **Song style + theme (free-form input; only needs to be filled in when Q1 is B "No, generate one for me"; leave blank if A)**: What style and theme do you want for this song? (e.g., `fresh youthful feel, about first love and farewell` / `dark cool feel, about loneliness and rebellion`) — **no matter what is filled in, the generated music is always locked to the broad POP context** (pop / rap / hip-hop / R&B).
3. **Vocals (gender + language)**: Which vocals are to be used / generated? **Choose gender (male / female) + singing language** (Chinese / English / Korean / …). (If audio was uploaded, it can be auto-detected to prefill; the user can change it.)
4. **Portrait / styling reference image present or not**: Is there a face reference image (or a &lt;15s video) / a styling reference image?—**As long as there is a portrait reference image → the protagonist's gender and ethnicity always follow the portrait reference image; Question 5 "Ethnicity" is left blank/skipped**; if not, fill in Question 5 as usual.
5. **Character ethnicity (free input; fill in only when Question 4 is "no portrait reference image"; leave blank if there is a portrait reference image)**: What is the protagonist's ethnicity? **Recommend primarily based on the language from Question 3** (Chinese→Asian, English→Western, Korean→Asian, Spanish→Latino…); you may enter / correct it yourself.
6. **Subtitles**: Does the final video need subtitles? Yes→choose a language (Chinese / English / …); No→no subtitles. (Subtitles are only overlaid in Stage 5; generated visuals must always contain zero text)
7. **Tone preset**: **A Cool sculptural / B Warm film / C Sharp flash grain**, choose one, uniform across the whole film.
8. **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 / 4:3 / 9:16?
9. **Resolution**: 480p / 720p / 1080p? (The gritty feel comes from grain and color grading, not from low resolution)

> Conclusion: **Protagonist gender = vocal gender**; **Protagonist ethnicity = with a portrait reference image→always follow the reference image (leave Question 5 blank) / without a portrait reference image→recommend based on the Question 5 language and confirm with the user**. **Duration is not asked separately—it is set automatically by [Clip Selection] later. All 9 questions above are asked in this same card, one thing per question, each with its own clean options; do not blend them into each other's options, and do not open another card.**

**Audio ready / Music confirmation (before slicing)**:

- **Audio available**: Use the user-uploaded audio.
- **No audio**: Generate a vocal song with lyrics based on the \*\*song style + theme + vocals (gender/language)\*\* above (**always locked to a big POP context**) → **after generation you must pause and wait for the user to confirm the music is fine (they may request a redo/fine-tuning); only proceed to slicing once they are satisfied**.
  - **⚠️ When generating music, strictly separate the two fields "style control" and "lyrics"; don't let the model sing the descriptor words**:
    - **Style / theme / mood / BPM / genre / vocal gender / language** go only into the **style description / tags / style field (control items)**; **absolutely never put them into the lyrics field that will be sung**.
    - **Write the lyrics field separately as a proper song**: write it in the chosen **language**, keep it on the **theme**, give it a **verse/chorus (Verse / Chorus) structure**, and divide sections with structure tags (e.g. ``` [Verse]``[Chorus] ```); these tags are structural hints and will not be sung.
    - **Never sing the style/theme/descriptor words from the prompt (e.g. "youthful indie-pop, about growing up and farewell, female vocals, Korean") verbatim as lyrics**—those are control items, not lyric content.
    - If the tool has only a single input box, **clearly partition it**: first write the style tags, then write the real lyrics after a clear separator, and note "only the lyrics portion is sung; the style tags are not sung".
    - **The music prompt as a whole ≤ 1024 characters** (style control + lyrics combined); if it exceeds, condense the description and keep only the core verse+chorus sections of the lyrics—don't force it.

**Two mandatory steps after audio confirmation (before storyboarding and video generation)**:

- **① Audio slicing (+ get the lyrics)**: Slice the audio into **multiple consecutive slices, each ≤30s (split one by one according to the count/seconds of the corresponding videos; how long each slice is depends on what that part/scene needs)**, and at the same time **obtain the accurate lyrics** (**for internal lip-sync alignment/clip selection only; never write them into any prompt sent to models**)—for the lip-sync segments that follow, **each slice is fed as an audio anchor to Seedance 2.5 (sd2.5) to drive lip-sync**. **After slicing you must stop and wait for the user to confirm the slices before continuing.**
- **② Clip selection**: Ask "**Which segments do you want to use for this MV?**" and let the user choose. **Recommend choosing several consecutive segments (each ≤30s)** (for smoother transitions between segments); **recommend picking segments with rap/vocals/lyrics for lip-sync; purely instrumental segments (intro/beat interludes) default to no lipsync**. **Once the user has chosen, the duration is computed automatically (= the sum of the selected segments' seconds = the number of video segments); no need to ask "how long do you want it"**; the number of segments is determined by this step.

**Styling routing**: Depending on whether portrait / styling reference images were uploaded per inquiry card Q3, take one of the four cases below.

**Four cases (divided by what the user uploaded; in all cases, first "understand the styling direction + combine it with the preset styling languages below to expand into a complete written spec"—never discard the image and copy as-is. Unified flow: Seedream 5.0 Pro produces one 16:9 white-background 2K image with 4 different full-body outfits arranged horizontally → the user picks 1 to confirm → Lib Image expands the chosen outfit into a three-view sheet)**:

> **Iron rule: portrait/face reference images are never fed to the styling model (Seedream)** (Seedream only accepts **styling reference images** as image references); **the face reference image is fed only at the final Seedance video-generation stage**, together with the styling three-view sheet (locks the real face; **hairstyle is the user's choice: keep the reference image's hairstyle / switch to the three-view sheet's hairstyle**). **How the face in the three-view sheet is handled depends only on "whether a real portrait reference image exists": yes→cover the face with a pure-white circular sticker + a small Chinese annotation at the bottom-right; no→keep the face of the chosen Seedream outfit.**

- **③ Both portrait and styling reference images**: **only the styling image is used as Seedream's image reference; the portrait is not fed** + expand the spec → Seedream produces four outfit images → the user picks 1 → Lib Image produces the three-view sheet, **face fully covered with a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese text at the bottom-right: "Face provided separately · This image is for outfit-styling reference only"**; the portrait reference image is fed **only at Seedance time**, together with the three-view sheet (locks the real face; **hairstyle is the user's choice: reference image's hairstyle / three-view sheet's hairstyle**).
- **④ Neither**: use **the ethnicity + gender recommended in step one based on vocal language + gender and confirmed by the user** + expand the spec; Seedream produces four outfit images (no trailing strings) → the user picks 1 → Lib Image three-view sheet, **face not masked** (the chosen outfit's face is the identity and also serves as the Seedance face reference). Do not separately ask "protagonist gender/ethnicity".

Each preset styling language (align when expanding, **written in Chinese**; **overall tone: fashion editorial / supermodel off-duty street-style level of styling—relaxed, effortless elegance, absolutely not tacky / cheap / logo-stacked streetwear**; **only hold the clothing bottom line: private parts covered, no explicit exposure, non-erotic; midriff-baring/skin-revealing, slim-fit, and cropped are all fine**):

### Stage 4 · Seedance 2.5 video (one clip per segment, duration = that segment's audio rounded up then trimmed, generated one clip at a time; **prompts in Chinese**)

prompt structure (in order, written entirely in Chinese):
1. **Front-loaded style sentence** (take A/B/C per the preset, uniform across the whole video).
2. **Character assembly + character present in the first frame**: a three-view sheet locks the styling/figure + a face reference (**with a portrait reference image → use the original image/video to lock the real face; for hairstyle, ask the user first: keep the reference image's hairstyle / switch to the hairstyle from the styling three-view sheet, and write the choice into the prompt. No portrait → rely primarily on the styling three-view sheet, taking both face and hairstyle from it**). 3. **Lip-sync + audio wiring (segments with rap/vocals)**: **the upstream [Selected Audio Slice] node must be wired into the audio input port of the video generation (Seedance 2.5) node** — this step is a hard requirement; without the connection you get neither the lip-sync driving nor the BGM, and the whole segment is as good as wasted. Feed that segment's **≤30s music slice into Seedance 2.5 as the audio anchor**, with the character's **mouth shape/jaw fully matching the incoming segment audio** (`the character's lip movements fully match the incoming segment audio, jaw and lips precisely following the rhythm and flow of the audio, natural singing/rapping expressions`); **never write the real lyric text into the prompt** (lyrics are for internal alignment only); **for purely instrumental segments the character does not sing, but that segment's audio slice must still be wired in and kept as BGM** (`no singing, performance only`).
4. **Storyboard**: `Shot 1:… hard cut to Shot 2:…`, one continuous clip (duration = the segment's audio rounded up), **≥8\~9 shots/30s** (about one shot every 3–4 seconds), following the BPM. Each shot = shot size + camera movement (handheld/slow motion) + **performance beats (mandatory: rapper gestures/singing-dancing/lip-sync/grooving, no standing idle)**; on-beat hard cuts/flash cuts, slow motion as an accent.
5. **Movement**: `natural handheld movement with an organic breathing feel`; slow-motion shots `slow motion / overcranked`; one-take only as needed `one continuous take`.
6. **Audio + duration + zero text**: the generated footage **always has zero text/logo/no burned-in subtitles** (subtitles, if needed, are overlaid in Stage 5 in the chosen language). **Every segment is driven by the wired-in audio slice + kept as BGM**; **the prompt must absolutely never contain** `No BGM` **/** `no music` **/** `no audio` **/** `no sound` **/** `no music` **/** `no BGM` **/** `no audio` **or similar wording (it misleads and suppresses both the lip-sync driving and the BGM)** — the incoming music is always kept as BGM, never discarded, the original audio never modified, and **real lyrics are never written**; for purely instrumental/non-lip-sync segments just write "the character does not sing, performance only" (never write anything like "no music/no BGM"). **Duration**: each clip's **generation duration = the segment's audio rounded up to a whole second**, then trimmed in Stage 5 to the audio's actual duration. Negative prompt `text, letters, numbers, subtitles, logo, watermark, cartoon, illustration, stiff, rigid, frozen frame, mechanical movement` (compliance is achieved via **positive, modest wording**; the negative prompt **must not include** any adult/exposure-sensitive words to avoid triggering moderation, and likewise **must not include** things like no music/No BGM; do not block neon/glitch/surreal).

**Performance defaults** (injected per preset when the user hasn't specified performance; each shot ≥1 expression + 1 action, **in Chinese**):

- A: `a composed, oppressive aura, restrained minimal movements, slow unhurried gestures, sharp gaze, solemn stillness`
- B: `expressive, vivid performance, rhythm-driven choreographed movement, precise lip-sync, powerful gestures, dramatic energy, interaction with the space`
- C: `playful, provocative energy, seductive looks straight into the camera, casual loose dancing, sweaty party grooving, crisp improvised gestures`
- Universal anti-stiffness (add to every shot): `chest gently rising and falling with breath, subtle movement of hair and clothing, expressions shifting slowly, light nods to the beat, jaw and lips genuinely moving during lip-sync, natural, composed, real-person-like movement`

> Generate one clip at a time, confirming each one.

### Stage 5 · Assembly (video_assembler)

Multiple segments joined in sequence, **each junction between two segments bridged through logic / shot language** (matching action / composition / camera-movement direction / subject position / mood, see the storyboard script) to make the cuts flow; add a single-frame white/black flash at segment cuts/beat-drop points; **pick one of the three text-overlay styles** (A brutalist large type / C black text on bright green / B minimalist), **overlay only, never burned into the generated footage**; logo PNG can be overlaid; **each segment's video is trimmed to that segment's audio's actual duration** (rounded up at generation, trimmed back here). **Music: do not take the original music asset as a separate track and splice it back in — directly reuse the music already embedded in each generated segment video** (i.e., the original audio that was fed in at generation and preserved as-is); joining the segments together with their embedded audio tracks forms a continuous soundtrack, with lip-sync fully matching the music and continuity across the whole video. **Subtitles (only if the intake card selected "needed")**: **overlay** subtitles on the final cut in the chosen language (Chinese/English/…) (overlay only, not burned into the picture); if not selected, no subtitles. Guide the export.

## Reference Data

### BPM → Shot count per 30s (hard floor ≥8\~9, about one shot every 3–4 seconds)

> Choruses are usually denser than verses; segments shorter than 30s scale proportionally (about one shot every 3–4 seconds); **no matter how many shots, generate them all in one go within a single ≤30s clip**. **The more shots there are, the simpler and clearer each shot's performance action should be (one gesture / one lip-synced line), to avoid overstuffing a single clip and making it mushy.**

### Scene Tendencies (each preset picks just one type; all are wide shots with no people, each with one mood/surreal element)

- **A Cold-toned sculptural**: church/temple sacred space · cold-lit void with a single light column · brutalist megastructure/industrial negative space · empty arena · wasteland snowfield. Mood elements: cold light, sacredness, vast emptiness, negative space.
- **B Warm-toned filmic**: hometown block/community block · single-space retro performance set · white institutional corridor · single-space hotel room · car-culture night street · dramatic symbolic tableau. Mood elements: community warmth + one dramatic symbol.
- **C Sharp-flash grain**: club/warehouse dance floor · airport/cabin transit · dinner glam interior · paparazzi-chase street · house-party small room · Y2K interior/car interior. Mood elements: party energy + one flash/surreal touch.

> "Mood/surreal element" = fog, strong light beams, oversized scale, out-of-place objects, flare spots, etc. (no gore, no realistic violence). If a longer video runs short of locations, the same location may be revisited (with different lighting/treatment).

### Notes
- **Before shooting, use only a single question card: all 9 questions live on this one card, each question asks only one thing, and each gets its own clean options (never blend two questions into the options of one question, and never split across multiple cards)**: ① Audio presence (only two options: have it / don't have it — generate it for me) → ② Song style + theme (free input, filled only when there's no audio, left blank when audio exists; generated music is always locked to big POP) → ③ Vocals (gender + language) → ④ Whether there are portrait/styling reference images → ⑤ Character race (free input, filled only when there's no portrait reference, left blank if there is; recommended based on language) → ⑥ Subtitles (choose the language if wanted) → ⑦ Tone preset A/B/C → ⑧ Aspect ratio → ⑨ Image quality. **Conditional questions display as usual; leave blank/skip if not applicable; don't merge them into other questions. Protagonist gender = vocal gender; duration is not asked, it's determined by the selected segment.**
- **When there's no audio, first generate music → must pause and wait for the user to confirm the music is satisfactory → then slice** (after slicing, confirm the slices, then select segments).
- **When generating music, style/theme/BPM/vocal gender/language go only into the "style control/style field"; the lyrics field separately contains a proper verse-chorus**; **never sing the style/descriptor words verbatim as lyrics** (if there's only a single box, clearly partition it within the box + note that style tags are not sung); **music prompt ≤ 1024 characters** (style + lyrics combined).
- **All prompts are written in Chinese** (MJ / Lib Image / Seedance alike); **parameters and code are not translated** (`--sref`/`--ar`/`--v`/`--no` flags, seed numbers, ratios).
- **Outfit/character baseline (reasonable is enough, see 【Outfit/Character Baseline】)**: cover private parts, no explicit exposure, no see-through fabric over private areas, non-pornographic and non-sexualized; **exposed waist/midriff/shoulders/legs, slim-fit, and short styles are all allowed, no need to cover them completely**; **characters' skin is clean, no tattoos, no piercings** (no ear studs/nose rings/lip rings; rings/necklaces/sunglasses/chains OK); **if a skirt is worn, Seedance adds "same-color-scheme anti-exposure safety shorts under the skirt"**; express outfit compliance with positive words, zero exposure-sensitive words in the negative area; neutral words like tattoo/piercing may go into the negative.
- **Scene images**: created immediately after the styling three-view is confirmed — the user picks which scenes to use → images are generated immediately, 1 group of 4 images per location (multiple groups at once allowed), **choose 1 of 4 per group**; multiple shots at the same location share the selected 1 image.
- **Outfits/styling locked unchanged throughout the film**: in generated videos, characters' outfits/styling stay consistent and must not change (every prompt emphasizes consistency with the three-view); exception only when the user explicitly requests an outfit change.
- **Every shot must have performance content** (gestures/singing-dancing/lip-syncing/grooving), no blank standing empty shots; when there are many shots, keep each shot's performance simple.
- **Audio anchor + lip sync + duration (core)**: the audio (uploaded or generated vocals with lyrics, vocal gender = protagonist gender) is first sliced into consecutive ≤30s slices (divided according to the corresponding number of videos/seconds; confirm after slicing) + **get the exact lyrics (for internal alignment only, never written into the prompt)** → the user selects segments to determine segment count/duration; **when generating video, the upstream 【Selected Audio Slice】 node must be wired to the video generation node's audio input port (mandatory)**, and that slice serves as the audio anchor fed to Seedance 2.5 to drive lip sync; the prompt explicitly states "lip sync matches the incoming audio", **does not write real lyrics**; **the incoming music is kept as BGM, not discarded, and video generation does not modify the original audio**; **the prompt must never contain wording like No BGM / no music / "no music" / "no audio" (misleading, and it would suppress lip sync and BGM)**; **generated duration = round the audio up to whole seconds → trim to the audio's actual duration**; for purely instrumental segments the character doesn't sing, but the slice is still wired in as BGM.
- **Final music assembly**: directly use the **built-in music** of each generated video segment, joined in sequence; **do not take the original music as a separate track and stitch it back in**; **subtitles are overlaid in Phase 5 in the selected language only if "needed" was chosen** (not burned into the generated footage).
- **MJ prompt ≤ 1024 characters**; **quality defaults**: MJ adaptive, Lib Image standard quality.
- **Styling image = Seedream 5.0 Pro**: one 16:9 white-background 2K image with 4 distinct full-body outfits arranged horizontally, no trailing text string, choose 1 of 4 (the chosen one is locked as the film's main styling); **three-view = Lib Image**, expanded from the selected styling; **when generating the three-view, the upstream 【Selected Styling Image】 node must be wired to Lib Image's image reference input port (mandatory; without the connection it will drift into a different set of outfits)**; within one image, 16:9, full-body left/center/right three views, **no head close-ups / big-head expression three-views**; with portrait reference → cover the face with a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese text in the bottom-right corner "Face provided separately · This image is for outfit styling reference only"; without → keep the face of the selected Seedream styling.
- **Hairstyle**: when there's a portrait reference image, before generating video ask the user "use the hairstyle from the reference image / from the styling three-view", and write the prompt according to the choice; without a portrait reference, base the hairstyle primarily on the styling three-view.
- **Face reference images are not fed to MJ, only fed to Seedance**; only styling reference images serve as the MJ image reference.
- Don't write real singer names / real MV titles as prompts — reproduce the aesthetic, not the specific work; use descriptive words for MJ style anchors.
- Scene images uniformly use **Midjourney V8.2** with fixed seed `--sref 5822120099`; **styling image = Seedream 5.0 Pro (2K, no trailing text string, no seed)**; three-view = Lib Image; video = Seedance 2.5.
- When the user only wants a segment/single shot, or has already specified scenes and styling, proceed as needed without breaking the rules: each segment ≤30s with duration = trimmed to the audio rounded up / lip sync matches the audio / no real lyrics / don't modify the original audio / use the generated videos' built-in music without piecing in a separate track / outfits locked throughout / protagonist gender = vocal gender / ≥8\~9 shots per 30s / zero text / performance / Chinese prompts.
