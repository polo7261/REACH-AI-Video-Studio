## Facial Micro-expressions and Ink-Medium Emotional Externalization (micro ink-wash detail iron rule)

- **Everyday and standoff dialogue scenes (emotional micro-expressions)**: slightly furrowed brow tips / flowing glances / taut jawline, with even, exquisite gongbi fine lines capturing a warm, classical grace.

- **Ultimate-move collisions / outbursts / hit moments (extreme emotions)**: teeth clenched tight, masseter muscles contracting violently, veins bulging at the forehead temples (bulging facial veins), pupils shrinking to pinpoints or bursting with highly self-luminous Five Elements iridescence.

- **Facial ink-medium fusion (sweat/tears/blood must never be rendered realistically)**: reshaped through expressive ink-wash media—half-dried old-ink dried blood-tear streaks at the eye/mouth corners (viscous dry ink-wash trails), flying-white dry-brush scratch marks bursting along the bone structure, ultra-high-frequency splattered ink dots (kinetic ink splatters), heavy-shadow scorched-ink blotches—externalizing emotional outbursts and physical pain as thick-and-light ink tearing across the rice paper.

## Dynamic Coupling of Painting Style and Motion Tension (core iron rule, the painting style must never remain static)

Dynamically scale the "brush-and-ink tension" according to camera movement speed and kinetic force:

- **Static/slow camera movement (dialogue scenes / mood empty shots / extremely slow push-ins, pull-outs, and pans)**: refined gongbi edge outlining, thin and even lines with crisp edges, warm and elegant ink tones, preserving the delicate texture of rice paper—an Eastern, scholarly elegance and ethereal airiness.

- **Ultra-fast camera movement / powerful strikes (Whip Zoom / Whip Pan / fist-and-foot impacts / move-burst instants)**: the painting style transforms in an instant—extremely rugged, frenzied scorched-ink dry brush + flying-white hatching, lines suddenly thickening, dry-cracked brush-tip edges, masses of splattered black ink dots—hand-drawn keyframe artistry and dynamic destructive force with extreme visual impact.

## Three Tiers of Ink-Wash Splash-Color Ratio (Light-Dark Ratio)

- **Non-combat / Artistic Empty Shots (Highest Aesthetic of Dramatic Scenes · Three-in-One)**: fine gongbi detailing 35%–40% (subjects / key props / near-view tree-and-stone edges in ultra-fine gongbi outlining to preserve warm, moist detail) + artistic blank space 35%–40% (large areas of pure xuan paper left blank; clouds, mist, rain, and distant water in light-ink gradient blur for "solid-void interplay"; strictly forbidden to fill the background completely) + classical freehand splash color 20%–25% (only distant mountains / bamboo grove edges / waterfall flanks dotted with extremely light mineral colors — azurite, malachite, light ochre, mineral pale gold; ink divided into the five tones "scorched, thick, heavy, light, clear"; soft light and shadow rejecting hard-edged strong light).
- **Regular Combat / Drama-Action Transition**: balanced ratio of approx. 50%–60% black-white-gray base + 40%–50% splash color; background landscape in large-block splashed-ink, visual focal points / terrain layers layered with peacock blue or malachite green splash color; strong light-dark contrast (a beam of strong light pierces scorched-ink storm clouds onto the center of the battlefield, heavy black-white ink breaking the breathing rhythm).
- **Climactic Showdown / Five-Element Grand Collision**: color-dominant + heavy-ink anchoring, approx. 30% scorched-ink skeleton + 70% explosive layered splash color; high-saturation cinnabar red / bright gold / azurite splashed in large areas and colliding fiercely, yet structural lines outlined with rough, forceful scorched and leftover ink (**ink-bone, colored-dress**); deep ink and heavy tones control the center of gravity to prevent color overload from looking cheap; extreme high-contrast light-dark, light and shadow like lightning tearing pitch-black xuan paper.

## Dynamic Planning of Shot Durations (Solving the Pain Point of a Uniform 5s Duration)

**It is strictly forbidden to apply the 5s default duration wholesale**; each shot duration must be set dynamically and explicitly according to action intensity / information density / dialogue length. **Bear in mind the 4s hard minimum duration limit; strictly control every shot within 4s–12s, and absolutely forbid any ultra-short shot below 4s.** Matching rules:

- **Ultra-fast combat / Instant move switching / Close-ups (fast push-in / single heavy strike / teleport / flash-white instant)**: 4s–6s (emphasis on explosive impact; short durations preserve high-frequency, compact combat force).
- **Combos / Move-interlocked deadlock grappling / Ultimate head-on collisions**: 6s–9s (reserving ample screen-growth time for complex momentum changes / multi-hit strikes / particle bursts / camera push-pull).
- **Dialogue / Momentum-building dramatic scenes / Freehand empty shots / Demon-beath breath grand detonation**: 9s–12s (a single dialogue line of ≤10 characters calculated at 2–3 characters per second to preserve lip-sync duration; dramatic scenes / artistic landscapes with slow pans / spreading cloud-mist reserve breathing stretch).
