# Hirokazu Kore-eda's Film Aesthetics

> 🔴 **Please strictly follow this SKILL step by step.** Version: 1.5.2
>
> Underlying DNA (unconditional, carried by every single shot)

1. 🔴 **Natural diffused everyday light as the key light (Kore-eda's signature, most important)**: Light is "the light this space naturally has" — daylight coming through windows, diffuse light under the eaves, soft light filtered through paper windows/sheer curtains, the warmth of a single indoor lamp. Light sources are natural, their direction discernible but not dramatized, **never pursuing backlight bloom/overexposed signature looks**. Faces are lit evenly and softly with clearly readable features; shadows retain detail and air. **Plain, not high-key**: neither deliberately brightened toward the bright side nor darkened into low-key, restoring the true luminosity the eye sees in everyday spaces.
2. 🔴 **Plain transparency + breathing airiness**: The image overall is plain, clean, and transparent, with the depth and airiness of a living space, but **no soft-focus filter, no halation bloom, no hazy light fog**. Clear but not cold and hard, soft but not mushy. Eliminate all plastic feel of digital hard-edged sharp HD documentary, and all dreamy saccharine softness — what we want is "a sober, believable everyday life."
3. **65mm film clean texture (must be clean, not dirty or aged)**: A fine, high-resolution sense of film density + extremely clean, transparent layering; colors natural and true, with the warmth and thickness of film but **absolutely no coarse grain, no dirt, no aging, no retro-nostalgic filters**. The essence of 65mm is "film's warmth + extremely high cleanliness and detail retention" — grain so fine it is almost invisible, clean shadows, rich layering. What gets removed is digital harshness and cheapness, not clarity.
4. **Iron rule that warm tones are allowed (opposite to Iwai)**: **Warm colors are not forbidden**. Kore-eda's everyday life inherently contains much warmth — wooden floors and furniture, the straw yellow of tatami, the steam and oily sheen of meals, warm evening lamps, summer skin. Warm color is the body temperature of life; when it appears naturally, keep it, with no cool-tone correction. But **warm without being cloying or exaggerated**: it is "real warmth in life" rather than "an orange-yellow film produced by color grading"; the whole image still rests on a natural neutral base, with warm colors as breathing points for the space and the emotion.
5. **Medium contrast + rich layering**: Contrast is natural and true; highlights and shadows both have detail and layers; shadows are clean, not murky, neither crushed black nor deliberately brightened, restoring the true light-and-shadow distribution of the space.
6. **Medium depth of field, readable space**: No extreme shallow-focus softening. The background retains recognizable living environments (rooms, streets and alleys, courtyards); depth of field serves the "person in a space" relationship, rather than blurring the whole background into bokeh. The characters and the living spaces they inhabit are equally important.
7. 🔴 **Emotional core: everyday texture wrapping restrained emotion (Kore-eda's soul — must be held firmly)**: This is Kore-eda's most essential quality — the camera is a gentle observer, quietly gazing at ordinary people eating, walking, talking, staying silent. Emotion is **hidden in everyday actions and empty space**, never externalized, never melodramatic, never emphasized with close-ups. Beneath the surface flow family bonds, life and death, absence and companionship, the passage of time and unspeakable regret. ⚠️ It must never become tear-jerking melodrama, nor cold, detached documentary — the difference lies entirely in this layer of "restrained tender gazing": the camera loves these people but keeps a proper distance, letting the audience see and feel for themselves.
8. **Let the everyday flow naturally**: The steam of meals, laundry swaying in the wind, children running down the hallway, cicadas and distant sounds. Time flows at the rhythm of life itself, neither hurried nor paused.
9. **Scope**: Only the everyday-realism branch; no genre-style dramatic conflict / heavy-plot spectacle.

## Texture Tail Words (Included by Default in Every Image Prompt)

`Clean 65mm film texture, colors natural and true with film's warm density (clean and transparent, no dirt, no aging, no retro filter), natural diffused everyday light, softly and evenly illuminating clearly readable features, warm tones may be naturally retained (wood tones/warm lamps/skin tones, no cool-tone correction), plain and transparent, with living-space depth and airiness (no soft-focus filter, no halation, no light fog), medium contrast with rich layering, clean shadows neither crushed black nor murky, medium depth of field with a recognizable living environment in the background, the restrained plain warmth of an everyday gaze; no backlight bloom or overexposure, no soft-focus haze, no high-key brightening, no coarse grain or aged dirtiness, no shallow focus blurring everything into bokeh, no melodramatic theatrical lighting`

## Workflow

1. **Unified entry questionnaire (single option-card question box)**: Ask all at once: ① Duration (single shot ≤15s / single episode 30–60s / multi-episode several minutes / Custom) ② Execution mode (fully automatic / manual confirmation) ③ Video aspect ratio and resolution (default 16:9; options 4:3 / 16:9 / 21:9 / custom input) ④ Dialogue language ⑤ The 3 core elements (living space / family relationship / everyday object anchor) ⑥ Whether a story outline already exists (if so, ask the user to provide it). All pop up as option cards and are written to memory. ⚠️ Only "information gathering" is merged into this box; the **generation actions** for the moodboard and story must wait until the user has answered and be executed in steps 2 and 3, not merged into the questioning.
2. **Style confirmation (generation action)**: Based on the 3 core elements from the entry → generate a moodboard (image generation capability, default 16:9, a single fused image filling full bleed; white borders/collages/panel grids forbidden) → save as the style anchor asset and record the archive marker.
3. **Story (generation action)**: If an outline was provided at entry, follow it and only layer on Kore-eda stylization; if there is no outline, generate from the 3 elements (family / everyday life / life-and-death parting / the passage of time / restrained bonds). 🔴 **Must write sufficient everyday dialogue/narration; strictly forbidden to shoot everything as a silent film with no lines**: Kore-eda's "restraint" means the dialogue is plain and lifelike, without dramatic melodramatic lines — **it does not mean not talking**. He makes heavy use of dinner-table chit-chat, domestic murmurs, and off-topic everyday conversation, which is precisely where his flavor lies. Balance the tone by "speak fully when speech is due, leave silence where silence is due": scenes like the dinner table / spending time together / conversation must have lifelike dialogue written, and only scenes of solitude / gazing / transitions / the passage of time keep silence and empty space. Silent shots must be deliberate and must not be a lazy way to skip writing dialogue. ⚠️ When generating the story and shot list, check scene by scene: scenes that should have dialogue must not be left empty.
4. **Assets + shot list (produced together, confirmed together)**:

Scene images are single images, never split-screen. 🎨🔴 **When generating scene images, the prompt must explicitly @moodboard (the style anchor asset saved in step 2) as a reference image** — this is a hard constraint, not optional; if missing, redo it. The multiple "no moodboard" constraints in this SKILL apply only to video/characters/props and **must not** be generalized to cover scene images. Add "people, human shadows, passersby, silhouettes, human figures" to negatives. Summarize named props into a single white-background prop composite sheet.
- **List the storyboard table again**: Based on the story and the assets already produced, list the complete storyboard (each shot: shot size + @character action + camera movement + environment + duration); each shot must @-mention the character name and reference the corresponding scene image.

## Character Iron Rules

- **Lock the texture, loosen the identity**: the clean 65mm film texture is a hard constraint; hair color/hairstyle/clothing/identity temperament respect the user's settings, with no Kore-eda-style corrective adjustments.
- **Skin is natural, real color**: overall natural neutral tone; skin tone is real and healthy, with color and lived-in texture (slightly warm is acceptable); no deliberate whitening, no grayish pallor or dullness, no plastic skin-smoothing. A Kore-eda face is "a real face from everyday life" — with detail, with age, with breath.
- **Model sheet ≠ final footage**: the model sheet uses a pure white background + bright, even studio front lighting (face fully brightened, facial details prioritized); never use backlighting/dramatic lighting. Layout is 16:9: left three views (front/side/back) + right large facial close-up (mandatory, cannot be omitted, must not be drawn as full body).
- 🔴 **Model sheet template (fill in as-is; do not alter the structure or the texture closing words; only replace the \[\] placeholders; if the layout falls apart or views are incomplete, fall back to the English version)**:

## Scene Cards (pick per scene, shared underlying DNA)

- **Home interior (signature)**: Japanese-style or ordinary residential interior, daylight diffusing through windows/paper windows as the main light, the warmth of wooden floors, furniture, and tatami, everyday objects cluttered yet orderly, real traces of living. Soft and even, rich in layers, warm tones naturally preserved, medium depth of field preserving room depth. Characters perform daily actions in the space (sitting, walking, being busy), not posed.
- **Dining table/kitchen (Kore-eda's soul scene)**: A family sitting around a meal or preparing food in the kitchen, steam and sheen of the dishes, tableware and chopsticks, homely warmth. Natural light from above or window-side, warm gentle tones, a relaxed everyday atmosphere, multiple people in frame, relationships unfolding in the space. ⚠️ Kore-eda's most iconic scene; be sure to capture "the warmth and ease of everyday life"—no posing, no contrivance, with sound, motion, and steam.
- **Corridor/veranda/courtyard transitional spaces**: Transitional zones between indoors and outdoors (hallways, engawa, doorways, courtyards), light transitioning naturally between bright and dark, characters passing through or lingering, often with blank space and the silence of waiting. Kore-eda loves these "breathing points" of space.
- **Everyday exteriors**: Streets/slopes/riverside/the way to and from school. Natural soft light on sunny days (not dramatic backlight) / soft diffusion on overcast days / warm evening light (warm tones naturally preserved, no cold-tone correction). Characters walk through real-life street scenes; environment and people are equally important.
- 🌊 **Seaside town/summer everyday life (signature, must deliver high quality)**: The bright everyday life of summer—blue sky, greenery, a sense of cicada song, white laundry in the wind, by the sea or on a slope or in a courtyard, strong natural daylight but **soft and real—no blooming, no overexposure**, warm tones naturally preserved (the warmth of skin, of wood), naturally full colors with a lived-in feel, medium depth of field preserving the environment. Characters move about/linger/spend time together within the everyday. Negative locks: backlight blooming, blown-out dead whites, hazy soft-focus filters, coarse grain aging, cold-tone correction, shallow focus blurring everything. Whenever the user mentions summer/seaside everyday life, apply this card unconditionally; after generating, self-check that the light is soft and real, warm tones are preserved, and the environment is discernible.
  - Full prompt (ready to use): `Hirokazu Kore-eda-style summer everyday scene, bright and gentle natural daylight (soft and real, never backlight blooming, never blown-out dead whites, no soft-focus filter, no light haze), blue sky and lush natural greenery, white laundry swaying gently in the wind, a Japanese residential courtyard or a seaside slope or a quiet street, warm tones naturally preserved (the warmth of skin, of wood, of sunlight; no cold-tone correction and no exaggeration into an orange-yellow cast), naturally full colors with the texture of life and the gentle density of film (clean and clear, not dirty, not aged, no vintage filters), medium depth of field with the background living environment clearly discernible (not shallow focus blurred into bokeh), medium contrast, rich layers of light and dark, clean shadows that are not dead black, fine 65mm film with a clean texture and ultra-fine, nearly invisible grain, a restrained, plain everyday gaze, the image carries the breath and warmth of life`

## Camera Movement (one per shot, meshing with emotion, quiet observation by default)

Fixed long-take gaze / slow lateral tracking / slow push-in (restrained, almost imperceptible) / fixed medium shot waiting / slight handheld with the breath of life (non-impact) / fixed-point observation. ⚙️ Fast camera moves, handheld impact follow shots, rapid push/pull, and jump cuts are disabled by default, enabled only when the user names them. Kore-eda's camera is a "quiet observer"—the less it moves, the better.

## Editing Rhythm (default: a single quiet observational long take)

- Default to real-time speed, longer shots, allowing complete time for actions and silence; fast cutting, frequent switching, and jump cuts are forbidden (enabled only when the user names them).
- A shot may linger for a long time on a single everyday action or a stretch of silence, letting time pass naturally.
- Shot-size variation range: **on hard cuts, adjacent shots must differ in shot size by a gap visible to the naked eye, avoiding "a cut that equals no cut"**—immediately adjacent steps (e.g., medium shot↔medium close-up, medium close-up↔close-up) differ too little to count as an effective cut; on a hard cut, the shot size must skip at least one step (e.g., medium shot→close-up, full shot→medium shot) to create a sense of contrast. But the **upper limit remains: no violent jumps** (spans as large as close-up↔extreme wide shot are forbidden). That is: hard-cut shot-size changes fall between "skipping one step" and "jumping two steps"—distinct yet not jarring. If the story means to stay quietly on the same shot size, then don't cut—hold one long take to the end rather than cutting to an almost identical shot size.
- Let an action/a conversation/a silence happen in full, without interruption or hurrying. Each shot carries a complete stretch of lived time, not a single action beat.

## Music (pure instrumental, no lyrics, default mureka)

- Temperament: restrained, warm, spacious, never sentimental; advancing through timbre and atmosphere rather than melody—music is the base tone of daily life, not an accentuation of emotion.
- 🎹 **Piece 1 · Everyday warmth** (piano/acoustic guitar + string underlay), three sub-pieces:
  - A · Family everyday: simple, warm motifs on piano or acoustic guitar, a warm string bed, a melody that feels familiar and uncomplex. For dining table/home/daily togetherness.
  - B · Flowing time: spacious single piano notes, generous blank space, sparse melody, breathing with time slowly passing. For walking/waiting/seasonal change/solitude.
  - C · Parting/longing: restrained, inward sustained strings, melody held back rather than released, the blank space itself is the emotion. For farewell/absence/remembrance/life and death.
- 🌾 **Piece 2 · Quiet blank space**: minimalist—single piano notes, ambient sound, or a nearly silent texture; weak melody, strong space, letting ambient sound (cicadas/water/wind) take the lead. For gazing/silence/empty shots/time passing.
- 🌿 **Piece 3 · Warm everyday groove**: acoustic guitar or piano with a gentle rhythmic feel (not strong beats) + warm and bright, with life's light, flowing motion. The only piece allowed a slight groove, yet still restrained—"lightness within ordinary days," not cheerful agitation. For children playing/summer outings/relaxed family moments.
- **Piece selection**: home dining table→A; walking/waiting/solitude→B; parting, longing, life and death→C; gazing/silence/empty shots→Piece 2; children playing/summer ease→Piece 3. Multiple pieces may coexist in one film, switching per segment.
- No strong drums/pop arrangements/heavy beat-syncing/orchestral explosions/sentimental string floods/tear-jerking climaxes/lyrics or vocals. Kore-eda's music always recedes behind life, never rushing ahead of the emotion.

## Post-generation Self-check (redo if standards aren't met)

Is the light natural diffused everyday light rather than dramatic backlit blooming (must be natural)? Any soft-focus filter/halation/light haze (forbidden; must be plain and clear)? Are the facial features evenly lit and clearly readable? Are warm tones naturally preserved rather than cold-corrected (warmth is allowed)? Is the texture clean and clear rather than coarse-grained, grimy, artificially aged (must be clean 65mm)? Do the shadows keep their layers—not dead black, not muddy? Does the depth of field preserve the living environment rather than blurring everything into bokeh? Is the emotion a restrained gaze rather than sentimental dramatization? Are the shots quiet and long enough—did they allow time and blank space? For summer/home shots: is the light soft and real enough, are warm tones present, is the environment discernible?

## Video Iron Rules

- 🔴 **One clip, multiple storyboard shots (forced by default)**: seedance runs up to 15 seconds per generation and natively supports multiple storyboard shots within one clip + hard cuts between shots—**do not use "one shot per clip + post-production splicing"**. Assess total duration from the story's rhythm; don't force it to 15 seconds: Kore-eda's pacing is slow, with long shots and few storyboard beats—first estimate the number of life segments (one segment = one complete piece of everyday time: one conversation/one action/one silence) to set shot count and duration (1 segment→8s, 1-2 shots / 2→10-12s, 2 shots / 3→15s, 3 shots). **Only split into multiple clips if it exceeds 15 seconds; run them serially, then splice using video compositing capability**. The prompt advances second by second; each shot = shot size + @character action + camera move + environment; shots are long, with hard cuts explicitly breaking between shots.
- **No BGM output**: video generation needs only ambient sound + dialogue; BGM is overlaid at the compositing stage. Kore-eda's ambient sound (cicadas/water/tableware/footsteps) is extremely important; the prompt must always state "keep only rich, real ambient sound and dialogue—no background music, no score"; add "background music, film score" to the negatives.
- 🔴 **Dialogue must not be omitted (cures the "all-silent-film" laziness)**: When writing storyboards and prompts, any shot involving character interaction/companionship/dining table/conversation must by default include lifelike everyday dialogue (plain, homely, not sentimental); do not shoot every shot as a dialogue-free silent film in pursuit of "restrained blank space." Only leave silence for shots whose design intent is clearly silence—solitude/gazing/transitions/passage of time and the like—and the silence must be intentional, not a lazy omission of dialogue. Write the dialogue content directly into that shot's prompt timeline, subject to the language lock below.
- 🔴 **Narration is uniformly written into the video prompt**: Narration is not handled separately via voice acting/TTS and not overlaid in post; it is always written as part of the video prompt and generated by the video model together with the visuals (treated the same as character dialogue). For shots that need narration, write the narration content directly into that shot's prompt timeline, labeled "Narration (voice-over):"; narration is likewise subject to the language lock below. ⚠️ Koreeda-style narration must be restrained and plain—not lyrical, not sentimental; better fewer than more.
- 🔴 **Language lock**: Every video prompt permanently declares "All character dialogue/narration is in \[selected language\]; even when the model fills in lines on its own, only this language is used; dialogue/subtitles/lip movements in other languages are forbidden" (write it regardless of whether the shot has dialogue); add to negatives "dialogue in other languages, mixed-language lip movements". Prevents the wrong language from popping up when the model adds lines on its own to shots without dialogue.
- 🔴 **Group/multi-person scenes: everyone moves**: As soon as the frame contains multiple people or a group (dining table, street market, classroom, family gathering, etc.), you must ensure that **everyone in the frame is moving naturally**—each with their own everyday actions (eating, talking, turning heads, walking around, being busy, small gestures); eliminate background figures standing rigidly motionless, frozen like wax figures/mannequins. In the prompt, specify the characters' actions individually or in groups, and permanently declare "all characters in the frame move naturally; no one stands frozen"; add to negatives "background figures standing frozen, frozen portraits, wax figures, motionless mannequins". ⚠️ Sole exception: stillness is allowed only when the user explicitly requests that everyone remain still or stand motionless.
- 🔴 **Skin de-griming (video stage only, prevents seedance/video models from degrading realistic skin texture into grime)**: Static images/model cards keep the true skin tone untouched, but the video prompt must do three things to prevent skin from getting dirty in motion—① **Brighten**: skin (especially shadow areas) brightened slightly by about one stop relative to the static baseline, with shadow areas clean and translucent (this is "localized de-grime brightening of the skin," not pushing the overall frame toward high-key; the film's overall brightness still follows the plain neutrality of the underlying DNA); ② **Purify skin tone**: skin tone even and clean, no mottled stray colors, no gray-green/waxy-yellow/dirty color cast, natural translucent complexion; ③ **Rein in excessive texture**: retain natural skin texture but moderately restrain over-sharpened pores and oily sheen, neither rough nor greasy—while still **forbidding airbrushed plastic fake faces** (de-griming ≠ airbrushing). The prompt permanently declares "character skin clean and translucent, shadow areas brightened without dirt, skin tone even without stray colors or color casts, natural skin texture neither greasy nor airbrushed"; add to negatives "dirty skin, filthy shadow areas, mottled skin tone, gray-green waxy-yellow color cast, greasy oily sheen, over-sharpened pores".
- **Character solidity and naturalness**: Characters remain physically clear throughout, with natural everyday movements—no posed staging, no looking into the camera, no performative acting.
