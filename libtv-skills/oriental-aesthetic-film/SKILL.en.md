## One xianxia-atmosphere base texture + one optional primary-color line (distilled in Stage 1, unified across the whole film)

One rendering base for the entire film (beautiful light and shadow, shallow-depth-of-field soft focus, cinematic-grade texture); **scene images apply a fixed style seed (**`--sref 4482956243 --profile baq4x24`**) to reinforce unified texture across locations, while consistency is further strengthened by repeatedly applying the shared style description text**; **the film's final tone takes the tonality of the user-confirmed set of scene images as the standard, and the finished video must come infinitely close to the soft-focus, soft-light texture of the scene images — the same soft-focus filter, soft glow, and enveloping mist, and must not be sharper or clearer than the scene images**, and character-image/prop-image generation must likewise align with the color tonality of the selected scene images. From the atmospheric base, distill one further primary-color line and attach it to the scene-image prompt.

- **Shared style description (universal base for both scene images and Seedance, applicable to any location; condensed version, in Chinese)**: `Eastern xianxia-atmosphere cinematic imagery. Soft natural light; evocative lighting effects such as morning mist, dawn glow, candle shadows, or moonlight; soft-focus hazy ambience; luminous, transparent light and shadow. Cinematic photorealistic texture, HDR, 8K detail, true-to-life material texture, unified, beautiful, translucent color tone.`
- **Architecture/environment clause (optional; append only when the scene genuinely contains architectural/courtyard elements or the user explicitly wants a palace-and-courtyard atmosphere; does not emphasize scale)**: `May include ancient-style architectural elements such as celestial palace pavilions, courtyard colonnades, and carved lattice windows as background; details are refined rather than emphasizing mass and scale contrast; light-and-shadow atmosphere takes priority over architectural scale.`
- **Scene-image add-on**: `Extreme wide/wide-angle empty-scene composition, showing open space and depth layering, foreground to background clearly visible with no shallow depth-of-field blur; pure landscape empty shot with absolutely no people`, appended after the shared description (+ the architecture/environment clause, if applicable), then followed by that location's own terrain/environment details (e.g., bamboo grove, misty forest, courtyard, streamside, etc. — see the scene list descriptions) and the primary color.
- **Seedance lead-in style-sentence add-on (video-only, not used for scene images)**: `Live-action cinematic film texture; a soft-focus filter and soft-light effect are mandatorily layered over the whole film, with texture aligned to the scene images; portrait shots use backlight or side light to sculpt contours; mist drifts through the frame throughout; slow-motion captures of wide sleeves and gauze drapery drifting in the wind; character makeup and hair-ornament details clearly discernible; the share of portrait framing further increased, with frequent extreme close-ups; camera movement smooth but shot changes use hard cuts only, with no transition effects of any kind.`, placed as the first sentence of the Seedance prompt, after the shared description.
- **Optional primary-color leanings (appended after the base description; pick one sentence or write your own)**: ethereal cool tones "pale jade, moon white, misty cyan"; gentle warm tones "apricot, lotus pink, warm golden candlelight"; blue-green landscape "mineral green, azurite blue, warm golden sunset"; dark demonic netherworld "blood red, deep black, sickly green miasma".

## Workflow (Pause for Confirmation at Each Stage)

### Stage 0 · Pre-Shoot Question Card (ask in order; may be listed all at once; whether question 5 appears is decided by the result of question 4)

**Do not ask about the script/text**; this skill starts directly from "atmosphere + identity" and unfolds from there, not depending on a complete script:

1. **Overall atmosphere/mood**: First ask the user for the desired atmospheric tone (e.g., ethereal and flowing, cool and aloof, gentle and aesthetic, bewitching and demonic, hot-blooded and chivalrous, etc.; multiple selections allowed or write your own). **This step is the master outline for the entire film** — it simultaneously determines the visual tone (scene lighting/shadow and dominant colors) and the emotional direction of the Stage 6 score, and must be decided first.
2. **Protagonist identity setting**: Ask the user for the desired protagonist identity (e.g., knight-errant, sword cultivator, rogue cultivator, immortal, sect master, demonic-path figure, and other Eastern xianxia archetypes; multiple selections allowed, write your own, or give a custom description). **Defaults to a single-protagonist portrait film; if the user later needs supporting characters/multiple characters, that is equally supported**, handled via Stage 1's multi-character batching mechanism, with no limit on headcount.
3. **Presence of a character portrait reference image**: Ask the user whether they have a portrait reference image they want to designate. **If yes, wait for the user to upload it; once received, determine gender directly from the image without asking again**; **if no reference image, add one more question about the character's gender** (male/female/or user-defined, e.g., neutral/androgynous, etc.) as one of the base pieces of information for Stage 1's originally designed character card.
4. **Whether narration/dialogue is needed**: choose one of three — (a) the user writes the copy themselves; wait for the user to input or upload the text; (b) I draft 1–2 concise slogans based on protagonist identity + atmosphere (style is not fixed; classical Chinese short lines or modern poetic short lines are both fine, flexibly chosen according to the identity's temperament; produced in Stage 1 together with the character card for confirmation); (c) not needed at all — pure atmosphere with no vocals. **If (c) is chosen, skip question 5 directly and move on to question 6**.
5. **Subtitles** (asked only if question 4's answer is (a) or (b)): yes/no; if yes, choose Simplified/Traditional/English/bilingual (added directly in Stage 6 per this choice, not asked again).
6. **Aspect ratio**: 16:9/4:3/1:1/9:16 listed equally with no recommendation; the user must make the choice. **This is the aspect ratio of the final video deliverable; it is a different matter from the fixed 3:4 default aspect ratio of the Stage 2 character styling images, and the two do not affect each other**.
7. **Duration**: 30s/60s/90s/custom (fill in the blank, e.g., 150s → round up to multiples of 30s and split into 5 segments).

> **Resolution is not asked; it defaults to 720p**. After asking all of the above questions in order (whether question 3 adds the gender question depends on whether there is a reference image; whether question 5 appears depends on question 4) → once the user has answered → proceed to Stage 1.

**Three Cases for character styling (the specific use of the reference image is asked in detail only when deepening the character card in Stage 1, not at Stage 0)**: **In every Case, reference images provided by the user are never fed as image input to Midjourney or Seedream for generation** — Case1: the reference image serves only as a **styling/costume reference** (face not locked) → after visually inspecting the reference image, Claude translates the costume/styling details into the character card's text description; Midjourney generates from pure text, no original face; the three-view sheet is a left large-head close-up + right full-body three views; Case2: the reference image **contains a clear face and needs face locking** → the original face image **is reserved directly for the Seedance side to use as an identity anchor** and does not enter any Midjourney/Seedream generation calls; **the three-view sheet outputs only the full-body three views (without the large-head close-up panel), with a circular sticker over the head + a small-text annotation in the bottom-right corner**; Case3: **no reference image** → fill in the spec for an original design based on the identity setting/gender, no original face; the three-view sheet is a left large-head close-up + right full-body three views, and the face is the identity. All three Cases require cinematic-grade casting and makeup/styling, avoiding short-drama influencer faces. If the answer to Stage 0 question 3 is "have a reference image", then in Stage 1, after receiving the image, one follow-up question must be asked: "Is this image mainly for locking the face, for locking the full-body styling, or for referencing both?" — and based on that, decide whether to go with Case1 or Case2.

**Xianxia ancient-style makeup/styling vocabulary (used when filling in the spec; not locked to any dynasty; by gender)**: Female `flowing wide-sleeve hanfu or Taoist robe, silk ribbon sash drifting, layered cloud-shoulder cape, jade or gold hairpin, flowing high bun, delicate traditional makeup with floral forehead ornament and soft lip colour`; Male `Taoist/cultivator robe with wide sleeves, waist sash with jade pendant, topknot with jade or wooden hair crown, refined understated makeup with clean brow line`; Common `real silk/brocade/gauze textures with natural drape, exquisite embroidery and hair-ornament craftsmanship visible in close-up, no contemporary fabric/cut/accessories`. Sect identity or personality may fine-tune the color palette, patterns, and makeup intensity (cool/aloof leans plain and elegant; demonic leans rich and vivid), without changing the styling baseline.

### Stage 1 · Identity and Atmosphere Expansion + Final_Video_Spec
**Do not parse the script**; based on what Stage 0 confirmed — **protagonist identity + atmospheric tone + (if any) portrait reference image + (if (b) was chosen) the slogan to be drafted** — proactively develop the character/prop/scene settings, **output the tables and pause once; generate no images at all**.

- **Character card drafting**: Based on the identity setting + the xianxia makeup-and-styling language library, draft the protagonist's character card (appearance / makeup-styling key points / temperament keynote); if Question 2 of Stage 0 involves multiple characters, draft a card for each character separately; if the same character is mentioned repeatedly, deduplicate and merge.
- **Automatic scene recommendation (core mechanism)**: **Automatically draft 2–4 scene recommendations based on protagonist identity + atmospheric tone** (e.g., "swordsman + hot-blooded chivalric spirit" → night-lit inn, bamboo-forest mountain path, stone bridge by the stream; "immortal + ethereal flowing aura" → celestial peaks above the clouds, Jade Pool, alchemy chamber). After listing them, ask the user to **confirm or add/remove**; do not require the user to describe scenes from scratch; the user may also outright overturn them and use whatever scenes they want.
- **Automatic prop recommendation**: Likewise, give typical prop suggestions by identity archetype (swordsman → girded sword / wine gourd; immortal → horsetail whisk / jade pendant; sword cultivator → natal sword, etc.). After listing, the user confirms additions/removals, and locking/non-locking is judged accordingly.
- **Slogan drafting** (triggered only if Question 4 of Stage 0 selects (b)): Draft 1–2 concise lines of copy based on protagonist identity + atmosphere; the style is not fixed (classical-Chinese short phrases or modern poetic short lines, flexibly chosen per the identity's temperament), given to the user for confirmation together with the character card; the confirmed copy is the text source for the Stage 5 voiceover and the Stage 6 subtitles.
- **Must stop and ask when information is insufficient; do not blindly fill in gaps**: Mark items in characters/scenes/props whose descriptions remain unclear as **⚠️Insufficient information**, list the specific questions needing supplementation, and only enter that item's design after the user supplies them; "complete" items are unaffected.
- **Output format**: First report "X characters / Y scenes / Z props identified" plus a one-sentence note on the atmospheric tone; a character table (No. / Name / identity tags / appearance & makeup-styling key points / status), a prop table (No. / Name / owner / appearance / locking judgment / status), and a scene table (No. / Name / indoor or outdoor / whether it contains architectural or courtyard elements / light-and-shadow atmosphere description / whether it is a dedicated empty shot / status). **"Whether it contains architectural or courtyard elements" serves only as a reference for whether an environment clause is appended to the scene image; it involves no mandatory aspect ratio**.
- **Prop locking judgment**: Core keepsakes / repeatedly appearing accessories or magical implements default to [locked]; one-off set pieces default to [unlocked]; when in doubt, default to unlocked.
- **Spoken language**: Defaults to the user's input language; only append one question if mixed/unclear.
- **Reference-image purpose confirmation** (if the answer to Question 3 of Stage 0 includes a reference image): Follow up with "Is this image mainly for locking the face or the full-body styling, or should both be referenced?", and determine from that whether to go with Case1 or Case2; **in either case, the reference image itself is never submitted as image input to Midjourney or Seedream** — styling information is translated into text and written into the character card, while facial information is reserved for direct use on the Seedance side.
- **Batch processing**: If any of characters/props/scenes exceeds 8, automatically split into batches of ≤8 each, in order of first appearance, noting the batch plan below the table; after each batch is done, pause once to ask whether to continue, unless the user says per-batch confirmation is unnecessary.
- **Final_Video_Spec**: Aspect ratio (Question 6 of Stage 0) / resolution (default 720p) / duration (Question 7 of Stage 0, one segment per 30s).

> Pause for confirmation (the three tables + status annotations + reference-image purpose confirmation + slogan draft (if any) + spec, all paused together in one go).

### Stage 2 / 2.5 / 3 · Character·Prop·Scene Asset Generation (three lines launched in parallel, confirmation order unchanged)

> **Among the three production lines (characters/props/scenes), the character line's "pick-one-of-four styling image" step may launch its generation call simultaneously with the prop and scene lines to save time**; but **within the character line itself it is a two-step sequential progression** (only after the Midjourney pick-one-of-four is confirmed can it be handed to Seedream to produce the three-view sheet; the confirmation cannot be skipped via parallelism) — what are mutually independent are the three lines relative to each other, not the two steps inside the character line. **The order of showing results to the user and confirming them one by one still proceeds as 2→2.5→3**; parallel launching does not disrupt the confirmation rhythm. Any category exceeding 8 follows the Stage 1 batch plan, pausing once after each batch to ask whether to continue.

**Stage 2 · Character design (Midjourney produces the pick-one-of-four → Seedream produces the three-view sheet; for specific prompt construction see the "Fixed Seeds & Image Generation Specs" table)**: For each character, write a character card (including styling details: brows & eyes / lip color / huadian / hair-ornament craftsmanship; if the user has a styling reference image, Claude visually translates it into the character card's text description — it is not used as image input) → Midjourney generates a pick-one-of-four for confirmation (labeled 1 top-left / 2 top-right / 3 bottom-left / 4 bottom-right) → switch to Seedream and generate the three-view sheet using the chosen image as reference → check item by item whether the face / styling / wardrobe match the chosen image; if there is drift, regenerate to fix it → confirm all three-view sheets in one pass. Maintain the cast sheet; for costume changes, return to Midjourney to redo the pick-one-of-four.

> The four outfits must be pulled apart as complete ensembles — overall pairing + hairstyle + makeup must all differ; merely changing robe color while everything else stays the same is forbidden; characters' looks and styling must go for a cinematic quality, avoiding influencer faces / short-drama vibes; **the three-view sheet must be a single continuous white-background image, with no multi-grid feel of divided columns / borders / spliced panels** (at the pick-one-of-four stage, the standard Midjourney four-grid image-selection method, consistent with scene images, still applies; a single continuous canvas is not required).

**Stage 2.5 · Prop design (Lib Image; only needed for locked props; completely independent of character images/scene images)**: [Unlocked] props are skipped and left to be brought in via text at the storyboard/scene stage; [Locked] props are imaged one by one — write a prop card (material / pattern / dimensions / craftsmanship details) → **generate directly from the prop card's text description, referencing no character three-view sheet or scene image** → produce all locked props at once as single continuous pure-white-background canvases (front view + three-view details, no splicing seams) → confirm all at once (checking material, pattern, proportions) → for deviations, regenerate the side/back from the front image. Maintain the prop list. Props added midway are supplemented at any time.

**Stage 3 · Scene Tone Images (Midjourney V8.2, or V8.1 if unavailable; always apply the scene style seed** `--sref 4482956243 --profile baq4x24`**)**: Generate them together per the scene list (do not finish one location and then start the next): each location gets **1 group of 4 images**, **pure landscape empty shots with absolutely no people** (add `person, human figure, people, model, character` to the negative words). Prompt = shared style description + (optionally an environment clause when architectural/courtyard elements are present) + that location's own details + dominant colors + textual description of the aspect ratio + no-people negative words + a fixed tail string of `--sref 4482956243 --profile baq4x24` (the version is specified via the tool-call parameter as V8.2, or V8.1 if unavailable, and is not written into the prompt text); **keep the prompt concise (≤1024 characters)**. **Scene images are generated as panoramas per "location" — one group per location, reused across shots and segments**; no separate images are generated for close-ups/partial views — those are handed to Seedance to frame on the same panorama; number of groups = the number of deduplicated locations. **Once all are generated → pause once to let the user do pick-one-of-four per location** (labeled 1 top-left / 2 top-right / 3 bottom-left / 4 bottom-right) → only after all selections are collected proceed to Stage 4; it is strictly forbidden to move on without the pick-one-of-four; **any image in which a person appears must be regenerated**. **The tone established once these scene images are finalized becomes the tone baseline for the entire film**; all subsequent character/prop/video generation must align with its color quality.

### Stage 4 · Atmosphere Storyboard Table (must be produced before video generation; requires confirmation)
**Output all storyboards in one pass** (per the number of segments; do not stop after each segment), one table per segment, with columns: Shot #/Duration/Shot type (atmospheric empty shot/makeup & styling costume close-up/character medium-close shot/optional dialogue or combat/other)/Visuals (staging and makeup & styling presentation focus)/**Prop anchors**/**Scene anchors**/Sound layer (primarily ambient sound effects; if there is monologue/dialogue, note the original text and tone)/Movement. **Transitions are uniformly hard cuts; no transition column is set**. The "Shot type" column defaults to **atmospheric empty shots** and **makeup & styling costume close-ups** as its core, each appearing at least 1 time per segment; the remaining shots are distributed mainly as character medium-close shots, with dialogue/combat shots inserted as needed and no enforced ratio. For empty-shot rows, the Visuals column contains only landscape staging, the Sound layer may be left blank with just ambient sound effects, and Prop anchors are left empty.\
Below the table, attach a **continuity bible** (not part of the table): character identity/styling/held props/positioning/scene dominant color, lighting effect, and time of day/for multi-segments, a textual transition description from the previous segment's ending → the next segment's opening.\
Each segment: ≥6 shots per 30s (normally 6–8 shots, depending on the atmosphere), at least 1 atmospheric empty shot, at least 1 makeup & styling/costume detail close-up; if there are spoken lines, record the original spoken text for subtitling in Phase 6.

> **Pause for confirmation (one-time)**: all storyboards + styling images + three-view drawings + prop images + scene images stop together. **The storyboards are output all at once and stop only once; video (Phase 5) is the opposite—generate segment by segment, confirm segment by segment**.

### Phase 4b · Multi-Segment Continuity Continuation (key mechanism for 60s+, one entry at a time, pure prompt-based continuation, no video frames fed in)

After each seg_n is generated → stop and wait for confirmation → **record in text only** that segment's ending positioning/expression/lighting/shot type/prop state (the continuity bible already contains the basic info; supplement details here as needed) → write this textual info into seg_n+1's leading prompt, **do not feed seg_n's video or the extracted tail-frame images to Seedance as image input**; seg_n+1 is regenerated entirely based on the three-view drawings/scene images/prop images + the textual continuity description. Seam modes: **(A) Continuous action**—the text description continues the same action rhythm; **(B) Scene change**—the text description keeps character/makeup & styling/props/color palette/lighting consistent while the composition changes; in both modes the segments are joined with a **hard cut** at the assembly stage (no stylized transition effects of any kind).

### Phase 5 · Seedance 2.5 Video (this version is mandatory, one 30s clip per segment, one entry at a time)

Prompt structure: ① leading style sentence; ② **Assembly**—feed the scene images for the locations involved in that segment (selected per the storyboard's "Scene anchors") + each character's three-view drawings (locking face and makeup & styling; the pick-one-of-four image is no longer used) + each locked prop's three-view drawing (selected per the storyboard's "Prop anchors"; unlocked props are conveyed through text) + if an original face exists, the original face image additionally serves as an identity anchor, stating explicitly that "face and makeup & styling are taken from the three-view drawings, and prop forms are taken from the prop three-view drawings"; ③ **Storyboard**: `Shot1…hard cut to Shot2…`, one continuous 30s, shot count meeting the quota, at least 1 atmospheric empty shot + at least 1 makeup & styling/costume detail close-up, **shot changes use only hard cuts; absolutely no dissolve/cross dissolve/fade-in/fade-out or any transition animation or effect visuals of any kind, no exceptions**; ④ **Sound layer**: by default write only ambient sound effects; if Question 4 of Phase 0 selected (a) or (b), fill in monologue/dialogue per that shot's sound-layer division (mark the speaker, lip-sync), with the prompt in the user's language and the lines filled with the confirmed copy verbatim (self-written copy or the slogan confirmed in Phase 1); ⑤ **Movement**: for each shot, select matching entries from the "Shot Vocabulary" according to its shot type and combine them into a movement description, in which \*\*"soft focus soft light/backlight side light/mist" and "makeup & styling/costume detail close-up" are mandatory add-ons for every shot\*\* (to match the scene image texture), **portrait shots must outnumber empty shots; favor extreme close-ups**; if the shot is an optional action accent, append the "sword-riding/combat accent" entry from the vocabulary; ⑥ **Audio + text rules**: the positive states ambient sound effects (+ optional vocals); the negative appends the standard negative lexicon (already covering BGM/subtitles/transition effects/crowds/modern elements, etc.).\
**Performance**: each shot gets ≥1 expression + 1 micro-action, assigned according to the emotional tone, with cinematic acting quality. **Universal anti-stiffness (add to every shot)**: `natural subtle atmospheric sway, breathing on wide/close shots, micro head movement, natural blink, expression matching scene mood, robes/sleeves/ribbons/hair/mist drifting subtly`.

> **Segment-by-segment generation + mandatory confirmation gate**: generate one segment at a time; after each segment is generated you must stop and wait for confirmation before generating the next; strictly forbidden to generate continuously without confirmation.\
> \*\***Vocal fallback**: if the model's support for non-lip-synced VO is unstable, fall back to the assembly stage for TTS/recording overlay; all other rules remain unchanged.

### Phase 6 · Assembly

1. **Splicing**: all segments are joined with **hard cuts**; **absolutely no dissolve/cross dissolve/fade-in/fade-out or any transition effects of any kind, no exceptions**; the clips already contain ambient sound effects (+ optional vocals), no BGM, no transition visuals.
2. **Pre-compositing step**: ① **Add subtitles** `add_subtitle` (**this step exists only if Question 4 of Phase 0 selected (a) self-written copy or (b) an AI-drafted slogan; if (c) pure atmosphere with no vocals was selected, skip the subtitle step entirely**)—add directly in the language confirmed by Question 5 of Phase 0 (as an editable overlay layer, not burned into frames); **fixed style**: pure white text, **no outline**, font size **28px**, **single line, no wrapping**, font **Source Han Serif** (a serif typeface); ② **lay in a guofeng electronic soundtrack**—a **guofeng electronic (Chinese-style electronic) style soundtrack fusing traditional instrument timbres (guzheng/xiao & dizi flutes/bianzhong chime bells/guqin) with electronic beats/synthesized ambient bed, rising and falling with the shot atmosphere and emotional arc; describe only the style without specifying a concrete track; volume must not overpower the ambient sound effects or vocals; ③ image overlay**—title/timestamp/credits/logo PNG (not burned into the generated footage).
3. **Composite export**: flatten the composite into the final film.

## Reference Data

### Shot Vocabulary (transitions are uniformly hard cuts; no separate transition entries are listed)
Camera Keywords Makeup/costume detail close-up (core, mandatory) `macro close-up on hair ornament or embroidery craftsmanship, shallow depth of field, soft directional light highlighting texture` Slow-motion overcrank (No.1 signature) `slow-motion, flowing wide sleeves and hair drifting gracefully in the wind` Rack focus `rack focus from soft blurred foreground to sharp close-up on facial makeup or accessory detail` Backlit silhouette/soft rim-light contour `backlit silhouette with soft golden rim light, gentle lens flare` Orbiting camera move `orbiting camera revealing the full costume silhouette` Atmospheric empty shot (core, mandatory) `static or gently drifting shot of mist, floating petals, rippling water, candlelight flicker, no figures` Cloud-mist traversal (camera move, not a transition) `camera moves through drifting mist within a single continuous shot, revealing more of the scene` Medium-telephoto shallow depth of field `85-135mm portrait telephoto, shallow depth of field, softly blurred background` Soft focus & soft light/backlight or side light/mist (mandatory, match the scene image texture shot by shot) `soft focus filter softening the whole frame, gentle diffused soft light, backlit portrait with glowing rim light or side lighting sculpting the face, ambient mist drifting through the frame` Sword-riding/combat accents (optional) `brief flying-sword traversal within one continuous shot, close-up glowing sword-qi trail, camera gliding alongside subject` Overhead shot (atmosphere use) `high establishing shot of the misted landscape, soft atmospheric haze`

### Rhythm → Shots per 30s (hard floor of 6 shots, normally 6–8, all shot changes are hard cuts)

Rhythm Shot Count Notes Atmospheric empty shot 1–2 (must include at least 1 atmospheric empty shot) long take + slow movement; hard cut Makeup/character presentation 4–6 mainly extreme close-ups/close shots, soft focus & soft light + backlight or side light matching the scene image texture, interspersed with rack focus and slow motion; hard cut Voice accents (if any) 2–4 monologue/dialogue paired with close-ups, remaining shots for atmosphere building; hard cut Action accents (if any, optional) 1–2 sword-riding/sword-qi close-ups etc. interspersed between atmosphere shots; hard cut

> Every 30s must contain no fewer than 6 shots (sole exception: user explicitly requests a one-take), normally 6–8 shots, decided by the atmospheric rhythm; each 30s must include at least 1 atmospheric empty shot + 1 makeup/costume detail close-up; the remaining shots are mainly medium close-ups of the character; action/voice shots are optional accents with no mandated ratio; shot changes use hard cuts only.

### Scene Library (atmosphere-oriented, with architecture and scale de-emphasized)

Morning mist over the woods, bamboo grove path, moonlit courtyard, candlelit study pavilion (interior), sea of flowers/blossom-strewn stream bank, cloud-top tower (optional), snowy-night plum grove, mid-lake pavilion, streamside stone bridge, ancient well corridor, starfield and Milky Way, peach blossom dreamscape, cliffside overlook. Longer pieces may revisit the same location with different lighting/camera placement/time of day.

### Prop Library Reference (lock decisions)

Usually locked: the protagonist's carried accessories (hairpin/jade pendant/sachet), key keepsake tokens, regularly worn weapons or magical implements. Usually not locked: one-off set dressings, crowd background props. When in doubt, default to not locking.

### Notes

- Do not write real sects/works/actor names as prompts; the xianxia feel is carried entirely by descriptive wording.
- No modern elements in the frame (mandatory, see the standard negative-prompt lexicon); applies to all tools.
- Character pick-1-of-4 styling images are always made with Midjourney V8.2 (V8.1 if unavailable, fixed character-specific style seed `--sref 4937405977 --profile gt1hrhk`); character three-view sheets are always made with Seedream 5.0 Pro (generated from the selected Midjourney image); scene images are always made with Midjourney V8.2 (V8.1 if unavailable, fixed scene-specific style seed `--sref 4482956243 --profile baq4x24`); prop images are always made with Lib Image; videos are always made with Seedance 2.5 — none may be substituted, and the character and scene sref/profile pairs must not be mixed. All three generate no transition effects or text/subtitles in images/videos; both the character three-view sheet and the prop three-view sheet must be a single image with no stitching seams; character/scene images are produced as one set of 4 per round for pick-1-of-4.
- When the user only wants a fragment/single shot, or has already specified the scene and look, handle flexibly on demand as long as the red lines and core rules are not broken.
- Core differences from the narrative-oriented short-drama version: removes extreme scale contrast, megastructural anchors, mandatory three-layer parallel sound, strong-reversal narrative drive, and forced combat-shot variety; adds mandatory makeup/costume detail close-ups, mood-driven atmospheric empty shots, and Chinese-style electronic score; voice and combat action are downgraded to optional accents. Stage 0 does not ask about the script; instead it asks in the order atmosphere → identity → reference image (if none, follow up on gender) → narration needs → (conditional) subtitles → aspect ratio → duration; Stage 1 automatically recommends scenes and props based on identity + atmosphere for confirmation.
- Character design is two steps with two tools: Midjourney (character-specific seed) produces the pick-1-of-4, and after the user picks one it goes to Seedream for the three views; **the pick-1-of-4 does not link the user's uploaded original reference image** — the reference image is only visually translated by Claude into the character card text (styling info) or left to the Seedance side as an identity anchor (face info), and is never submitted as image input to MJ/Seedream; the 4 images MJ outputs in one run are 4 variants of the same character, not "4 characters stitched into one image."
