### 1. Skill Purpose

This Skill is dedicated to creating cinematic, dark-aesthetic comic-style dramas in the vein of "Len Wiseman." It transforms rough ideas or scripts into a visual style definition, character assets, scene settings, and director prompts for 15-second short videos compatible with Seedance 2.0, ensuring every frame carries the industrial-grade polish and oppressive emotional force of a Hollywood blockbuster.

### 2. Applicable Inputs

- **Theme inspiration**: e.g., "Return of the War God," post-apocalyptic survival, Eastern fantasy cultivation, period court intrigue, etc.
- **Short drama scripts**: written content with existing dialogue or episode synopses.
- **Visual preferences**: dark realism, hardcore CG, or a sharp anime style.

### 3. Steps

1. **Visual style confirmation**: First confirm with the user one of the three signature dark styles (realistic/CG/anime) and the aspect ratio (9:16 recommended).
2. **Creative and script development**: Distill hit-worthy elements, then write a 10-episode outline plus a detailed script for each episode (with word-for-word dialogue).
3. **Visual asset extraction**: Generate image-generation prompts (bilingual, Chinese and English) for characters, scenes, and props based on the "blue-black base, hard-light blade" principle.
4. **Video segment direction**: Automatically split the script into 15-second segments and output director prompts that conform to the Seedance 2.0 specification. Each segment includes mandatory director instructions, a shot-flow description, sound effect suggestions, and continuity context.

### 4. Output Requirements

- **Core aesthetics**: Eternal-night blue-black tones, hard-light blade-cut look, high contrast, low saturation, high material texture.
- **Composition rules**: Frontal shots are strictly forbidden; favor 15-45° side-angle or low-angle shots; strictly observe the 180-degree rule (never cross the axis).
- **Dialogue iron rule**: All dialogue must be preserved in full, word-for-word identical to the script; no additions, deletions, or changes allowed.
- **Prohibitions**: No subtitles, lyrics, or graphic text of any kind may appear in the frame.

### 5. Notes

- During video generation, each 15-second segment should carry no more than 75 characters of dialogue; if this is exceeded, additional segments must be added automatically.
- All image-generation assets must use a clean, dark (blue-black/deep gray) background to make character silhouettes stand out.
- Emotional expression should pursue "an icy eruption after suppression" and "the character's extreme sense of solitude."
