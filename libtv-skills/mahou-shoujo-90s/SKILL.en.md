# mahou-shoujo-90s

Use this skill when creating 90s hand-drawn cel-style, Sailor Moon-esque magical girl animation. Includes ritualized transformation, starlight battles, emotional facial close-ups, and a dual-layer dreamlike-realistic worldview. The sequence numbering system (V/C/E/ET/SF/P/B/DL/F and transformations A–E) is itself a craft with cross-section references and must be preserved as a whole.

> **Style homage, not exact imitation**: It draws on 90s magical girl genre aesthetics, **does not replicate copyrighted characters/names/specific designs/lines such as Sailor Moon**, and creates original characters; genre anchor terms in English prompts (such as magical girl anime theme) are style direction, and lines such as transformation declarations are `[Example]` placeholders, not to be copied from copyrighted lines.
> Environment adaptations: `generate_image_v3` (Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image 2, 2K) / `generate_video_v3` (Seedance 2.0, 720p, premium high-quality can do 1080p); `text_editor` → `write_file_v2`; `video_assembler` → `composite_video`; `resource_prepare_and_analyze` → `analyze`; `lyrics_to_song`/`text_to_instrumental` (Suno/Mureka) → `generate_audio` (character voice registered as key_element_audio for cross-shot consistency); `<<<image_x>>>` → `reference_nodes`+`{{NodeKey}}`; the "Seedance embedded audio retention" rule → equivalent constraint on this environment's generated video audio tracks (see Assembly). English prompt templates for the nine sequences + transformations A–E are in `references/prompt-library.md`; frame-level beat charts 4.1–4.8 and assembly specifications are in `references/editing-and-audio.md`.

## key_elements Design

**Character elements (element character, multi-character ensembles)**: Each magical girl gets her own key_element, recording:
- **Dual forms**: [Identity 1] everyday state (school uniform/hairstyle/temperament keywords); [Identity 2] transformed warrior state (outfit color theme/signature accessories ribbon-bow-tiara-gloves-long boots/exclusive runes/weapon). Use ImageToImage to keep both forms consistent across shots.
- **Color Identity**: Each warrior's exclusive primary color (e.g., Moon warrior = pink + silver, Fire warrior = red + orange, Water warrior = blue + aqua), used for cross-shot compositional color differentiation and ensemble color balance.
- **Friendship Role**: Emotional role in the ensemble (leader/guardian/wisdom/strength/gentleness), used for emotional layering design in group shots.
- Each major character binds/generates an exclusive **key_element_audio** to keep dialogue and combo-attack callout voices consistent across shots.

**Scene elements (element scene)**: Build a dual-layer "everyday + dreamlike" structure—everyday (school rooftop/night city rooftop), dreamlike (starlit cosmos/dream corridor/magical barrier); ensemble battle scenes additionally get exclusive wide-scene elements (e.g., `[Scene_Ensemble_Battle_Arena]`, noting multi-character composition: wide starlight stage/circular magic circle floor/multi-directional light beam sources).

**Villain elements (villain key_element)**: Required fields—**Threat Tier**: "Archenemy/Leader-class" or "Monster-of-the-Week"; **Dark Color Identity** (dark purple + corrupted gold / abyss blue + frost silver / blood red + pitch black, strongly contrasting with the pastel palette for confrontational color opposition); outfit and body keywords (exaggerated shoulder silhouettes/long floor-dragging dark cape/corrupted mutated star-moon runes/half-face-covering hairstyle/mirrored distorted structure echoing the magical girl outfits); temperament keywords ("coldly arrogant type" icy pupil color/slow-motion gaze/low-toned lines or "twisted maniacal type" split-open grin/asymmetric movement/alternating mockery and outbursts). Villain scene elements: exclusive dark domain (corrupted dark palace/rift dimensional space/darkness-eroded city ruins), noting tonal contrast with everyday/dreamlike scenes (warm pink gradients vs cold black gradients).

## Nine Sequence System (Storyboard Planning Rules, Cross-Referenced Numbering)

Below are the **storyboard structures and beat rules** for each sequence; corresponding English prompt templates are in `references/prompt-library.md`.

### Villain Entrance V-1~V-3 (2–4 shot standard sequence)
- **V-1 Silhouette Oppression**: Entrance as a backlit silhouette, showing only an exaggerated outline and glowing dark eyes; high-angle downward view or extremely low upward angle to create size oppression; background color temperature drops sharply.
- **V-2 Detail Reveal**: Slow push-in, revealing segment by segment from cape hem→waist→chest rune→chin→corner of mouth→one eye, **one-shot full frontal face reveal forbidden** to preserve suspense.
- **V-3 Line Declaration**: Extreme close-up of the corner of the mouth/icy eyes with the first line, in a calm arrogant or mocking contemptuous tone (≤15 characters), paired with a low voice key_element_audio.
- **Rules**: V-1/V-2 forbid showing the complete face (suspense saved for V-3 or even the battle climax); when the villain and girl share a frame, cold-warm color opposition zones must be specified; V-3 lines are calmly arrogant (MOTW-tier can have arrogant, short, brief taunts ≤10 characters); entrance uses low brass + dissonant string tremolo, and this must not be mixed with the transformation segment's starlight bells + rising orchestral—two distinct sound systems must not share a shot.

### Face-Off C-1~C-6 (Six-beat full sequence; MOTW simplified to C-1→C-4→C-5)
Distribute according to "oppression establishment→emotion brewing→clash probing→emotional eruption→composition flip→lingering resolution"; **simplifying into a single fight shot is forbidden**:
- **C-1 Oppression Establishment**: Strong diagonal composition with villain side high/girl side low, villain occupying about 60% visual weight, Dark Color Identity cold dark suppressed color temperature; **this beat does not allow the girl to actively attack** (stillness/retreat to build the oppressive base).
- **C-2 Emotion Brewing (Girl's Inner Close-up)**: Cut to emotional close-up (prefer E-1 tears or E-4 eruption threshold), **villain must not appear**; camera still or slow push-in, inner monologue allowed (≤10 characters, doubt/wavering); blurred background with defocused warm light foreshadows the flip.
- **C-3 Confrontation Probe**: same-frame medium two-person composition (two-shot), executing a "warm-cool color confrontational split" (left half: the girl's side in pastel warm light / right half: the villain's side in cool dark tones, with mutual interplay and permeation across the centerline covering ~10% of the frame); gentle push-in or constant-speed lateral tracking; may include the villain's second line of dialogue (mockery/declaration ≤15 characters).
- **C-4 Emotional Burst (Composition Flip Trigger)**: insert E-4 awakening burst close-up (gaze wavering → resolute peak frame, Color Identity light bursting from the iris, orchestra leaping suddenly from silence into full tutti); **this frame is the visual trigger point of the composition flip** — the camera pulls suddenly from close-up to wide shot, and the girl's posture shifts from defensive to proactive.
- **C-5 Composition Flip (Power Reversal)**: the villain-high/girl-low ratio is **completely overturned** — the girl leaps to an equal or higher position, and the Color Identity warm-light share expands from the initial 30% to over 50%; **camera movement must be a low-angle tracking shot** (tracking low-angle) to emphasize the ascending sense of power.
- **C-6 Afterglow Closure**: before entering battle, retain at least one emotional close-up or static two-shot as a buffer (optionally E-2 vow or E-3 friendship awakening gaze exchange), locking in the final emotional state and foreshadowing the motivation for battle; score with sustained string notes or piano underlay, not entering battle rhythm early.
- **Flip Node Rules**: the flip occurs only once (one-way progression, no repeated toggling); it must be emotion-driven (C-5 must come after C-4; a flip without setup is not allowed); in group standoffs the flip is led and triggered by the leader (Friendship Role: Leader) — C-4 focuses on the leader, and in C-5 the remaining members follow to form a rainbow array. Dialogue allocation: the villain's lines are concentrated in C-1/C-3, the girl's in C-2/C-6; C-4/C-5 are primarily visual and do not pile up dialogue from either side; C-2/C-6 must reserve at least 0.5s of silence or an extremely light score window.

### Emotional Close-ups E-1~E-4
- **E-1 Tearful Close-up**: before the transformation trigger / inner wavering during battle buildup / before the guardian vow; tears carry an optical texture (crystalline/prism/starlight refraction); **realistic physiological crying is forbidden** — maintain "beautiful tears."
- **E-2 Guardian Vow Monologue**: post-transformation declaration to the mirror / pre-battle stance declaration / group leader monologue; lines delivered slow and firm (≤15 characters).
- **E-3 Friendship Awakening Gaze**: emotional resonance of combo attack F-1 / emotional turning point / wordless rapport in a crisis; usually no dialogue.
- **E-4 Resolve Burst**: upgraded emotion-trigger version of transformation Shot A / the instant of resolve breaking past limits / re-ignition key frame; lines short and forceful (≤10 characters).
- **General Rules**: background heavily defocused (bokeh/defocus), with the subject's face and eyes as the absolute focus; score mainly piano/strings, with percussion or synth-driven dynamic rhythms forbidden (to distinguish from battle/transformation).

### Ritual Transformation A–E (Classic 8–10-Shot Structure)
- A Everyday trigger (holding the transformation device / resolute gaze / light overflowing from the chest); B Spinning ascent (rotating camera / body turning into a semi-transparent silhouette of light particles); C Costume formation (magic ribbons winding around the limbs, gradually solidifying into gloves and long boots); D Hairstyle change (hair billowing in the wind / a moon tiara hair ornament lighting up on the forehead); E Freeze Pose (low-angle hero pose / spinning freeze / a single-eye wink at the mirror). Per-shot core fields: visual scene (explicit scene ID reference); story beat and action (character body dynamics + classic line / transformation vow dialogue, referencing the character's element ID); camera language and composition (mandatory 90s terminology: extreme facial close-up / push-in zoom / 360-degree orbiting camera / soft-focus dreamy edges). Dialogue is concentrated in A (emotion-trigger inner monologue) and E (freeze declaration); B/C/D rely mainly on visual dynamics and sound effects without piling on dialogue. Shot lengths tend toward 8–15s (15s upper limit).

### Multi-Character Group Shots
- Group entrance: prefer the three-stage structure wide shot → individual close-ups → back to wide shot (Wide→Individual Close-up Round→Group Wide), with at least one emotional facial close-up per character.
- Group positioning and color: define each character's positional layer (foreground/midground/background); arrange left/right and front/back by Color Identity into a rainbow-style gradient (rainbow formation).
- **Friendship Combo Attacks F-1~F-4**: F-1 Emotional resonance trigger (close-up eye contact as multiple characters look at each other); F-2 Individual charging (cross-cutting as each character summons their own sigil/beam, each given ~1–2s close-up); F-3 Energy convergence (all beams/ribbons converge at the center into a rotating pillar of light or star-shaped burst, wide shot pushing in fast to the central burst point); F-4 Combined release and diffusion (the ultimate light wave spreads outward, cut to a panorama, everyone frozen in their own Hero Pose, surrounded by rainbow-colored light); lines kept short (≤10 characters/person) and distributed across shots in sequence rather than piled into one shot.

### Multi-Person Combined Transformation ET-1~ET-6 (7–10 shots, two or more transforming simultaneously)
Built around the axis "emotional resonance trigger → individual charging → light convergence → collective freeze," not a simple stacking of individual A–E sequences. **When to apply**: Solo transformation (A–E) = single character / emotion arising from one's own heart / solo instrument / standard ritual; Multi-person combined (ET) = two or more at once / emotion arising from group resonance / layered solos converging into an orchestral tutti / collective freeze replacing the individual Pose.
- ET-1 Emotional resonance trigger (cross-cut group facial close-ups of fatigue/wavering → resolve, final shot showing multiple characters in frame exchanging profile glances; **no transformation device appears**; lines are per-character tacit phrases ≤8 characters/person, distributed);
- ET-2 Synchronized device summoning (rapid cross-cutting with each character given a 1–1.5s close-up summoning their device, each Color Identity light overflowing simultaneously, edits aligned to the musical buildup beats of ET-3, final cut to a wide shot with everyone extending their hands at once; **each character's device sound effect fires independently, stacking successively in density with headcount rather than bursting simultaneously**);
- ET-3 Individual transformation progress (**flashback-montage-style compression**: for each character take 1–2 highest-visual-density moment frames at ~0.5–1s ultra-fast cuts, advancing in Color Identity rainbow sequence; **the full ritual is not shown**, only emotional peak frames extracted);
- ET-4 Light convergence center (wide shot of all members' light converging at the center into a rotating rainbow pillar of light / star-shaped burst, wide shot pushing in fast to the convergence center, orchestral tutti peak; **the visual axis of the ET sequence, the brightest frame of the whole segment**);
- ET-5 Group collective freeze (low-angle upward wide shot, all members frozen in their own Hero Pose, composed as a Color Identity rainbow array, light radiating outward in a constellation burst, leader at the visual center or slightly ahead, camera fully locked **holding 3s or longer**);
- ET-6 Declaration line rotation [optional] (quick cuts to each character's medium close-up delivering a declaration ≤8 characters/person, distributed; final shot cuts back to a wide shot where the leader delivers the combined declaration ≤12 characters, accompanied by a collective wink from everyone).
- **Planning constraints**: ET-3 compression principle (do not expand a full A–E sequence for each character, to avoid runaway duration / weakened group feel; a particular character may be given a slightly longer transformation close-up of ~3–4s for emphasis, but the overall rhythm remains ET-3 cross-cutting); Color Identity rainbow-sequence consistency (ET-2 summoning order / ET-3 peak-frame order / ET-5 placement arrangement must follow the same sequence); leader highlight (the leader's ET-3 peak frame runs slightly longer by ~0.5s; in ET-5 the leader's light is visibly stronger than the others'); **distinction from the F series** (ET = the transformation process itself; F = the combined attack after transformation; not interchangeable; when F directly follows ET in the same scene, the storyboard must clearly mark the cut point where ET ends and F begins).

### Season Finale Showdown SF-1~SF-5 (Five Beats, Deviating from the Standard Flow)
Driven by "ultimate breakdown → emotional sacrifice → miraculous awakening → ultimate purification → emotional lingering"; none of the five beats may be omitted (when runtime is tight, the number of shots within each beat may be compressed, but beats must not be skipped); mark "Season Finale Climax" to distinguish it from regular episodes; when media processes this segment, preferentially reference the "awakened form" key_element.
- **SF-1 Ultimate Collapse (4–6 shots)**: SF-1a wide-shot panoramic collapse overview (all members' Color Identity light dimmed/extinguished, battlefield dominated by Dark Color Identity, slow descent from a high-altitude overhead view), SF-1b one-by-one close-up group collapse sweep (tears/scars/shock/despair each about 1.5–2s, light reduced to mere residue at the bottom of the eyes), SF-1c leader extreme close-up (the residual Color Identity glimmer in the eyes is the only warm color, camera locked static, sound effects silent); Constraints: the soundtrack's "peak tension but orchestra not yet erupting" is held in suspension, no premature climactic brass, **at least 0.5s of silent frames must be retained** between SF-1c and SF-2a.
- **SF-2 Emotional Sacrifice (5–8 shots, highest emotional density)**: SF-2a sacrifice resolve close-up (tears dried or forcibly held back, exhaustion → calm conviction, a serene sense of "having let go", E-2 upgraded version, dialogue ≤12 characters, **gentle and resolute, not weeping**), SF-2b sacrifice act body language (sending the last magic energy/relic/Color Identity light toward companions or the leader, light flowing from the chest/hands along the frame's midline to the recipient, gentle Color Identity light beams connecting them, **explosion or shockwave effects forbidden**), SF-2c recipient reaction close-up (shock → tears surging → silent "I will remember you", E-3 variant one-way farewell gaze, final frame freezing on the last point where the two gazes meet), SF-2d sacrificial dissipation (the figure rises and dissipates into gentle neutral white light particles as in P-4 but **slower than the purification**, each particle carrying the weight of parting; soundtrack: the most fragile single-line piano melody, strings completely stopped), SF-2e brief battlefield stillness (wide shot of all members including the Boss briefly still, white light particles drifting, **at least 1s of stillness mandatorily retained**, no immediate cut to SF-3); Constraints: **no combat actions** in the entire segment (the sacrifice is an active choice, emphasizing agency and gentleness); in the all-members collective sacrifice version, SF-2b is replaced by a multi-shot intercut sequence of multiple characters converging their light toward the leader one by one (each about 2s).
- **SF-3 Miracle Awakening (6–10 shots, highest visual density)**: SF-3a power flowing into the inner sea of light (internal visualization inside the leader, abstract/semi-abstract sea of light, companions' Color Identity lights flowing in one by one to form a rainbow light pillar pouring into the chest, scene `[Scene_Cosmic_Void]` or a dedicated miracle space), SF-3b external awakening signs (hair strands rising against gravity, light intensity far beyond normal, new glowing patterns/sublimation ornaments appearing over the original battle attire, **more intense and without ritual feel** — overflowing power, not a ritual), SF-3c awakening freeze-frame debut of the brand-new form (the brightest light frame of the entire film, sublimated Color Identity light enveloping the whole body, new weapon/new headdress/new wings of light clearly visible, low-angle upward shot, Dark Color Identity cold tones forcibly transitioning into warm starlight), SF-3d Boss reaction (solo close-up of the first-ever "waver", slight frown/pupil tremor/aura-edge quiver, **extremely restrained, micro-expressions only**, obvious fear or panic forbidden); Constraints: the essential difference between SF-3b and A–E is the **absence of a preliminary ritual feel** (reusing the ribbon-wrapping/crown-lighting phrasing is forbidden, replaced by light bursts/pattern emergence/spontaneous attire sublimation); SF-3c requires an independent key_element variant built specifically for the "Awakened Form".
- **SF-4 Ultimate Purification**: identical to standard purification P-1~P-4 but with season-finale enhancements — P-1 enhanced (rainbow-shaped multi-colored light waves covering the Boss's entire body silhouette as a whole, not partially), **P-3 season-finale enhancement (mandatory, not optional)** (the Boss's true form must make its first appearance showing a complete frontal face; if the Boss has a tragic backstory, add a 2–3s memory flashback montage), P-4 enhanced (white light particles mixed with all companions' Color Identity spreading to fill the starry sky, "all colors returned to the universe"; the battlefield's complete warming from cold tones must take at least 2 shots to complete).
- **SF-5 Emotional Afterglow (4–6 shots, second-highest emotional density, cannot be omitted)**: SF-5a battlefield silence overview (color temperature warming/floating dust-like light particles drifting down/no one cheering/camera static), SF-5b mourning gaze (the leader gazing up toward the direction where the sacrificed one dissipated/eyes reflecting drifting white light particles/soundless tears/"relief and gratitude" rather than breakdown, E-1 variant background retains the sacrificed one's residual Color Identity light), SF-5c group silent mutual gaze (surviving members wordlessly looking at one another, "we're still here", E-3 closing variant "weary gratitude"), SF-5d return-to-daily-life shot【Optional】(if it is the series finale, the scene dissolves from the battlefield into daily life, using a starlight-particle dissolve effect); Constraints: soundtrack is the softest piano/strings lyrical epilogue, combat-rhythm sound effects must not reappear; if the sacrificed one has not truly perished, SF-5b's tears are adjusted to "smiling through tears", and after SF-5d append a reunion-hint shot (the sacrificed one's Color Identity light briefly appears in the sky for only 1–2s, no dialogue confirmation, leaving room for interpretation).

### Awakened Form key_element Design Rules
The visual pinnacle of the entire film; for every character who undergoes SF-3, a **dedicated key_element variant must be built individually**, placed in parallel with the daily/transformation forms:
- **7.1 Three Forms in Parallel**:[Identity 1] Daily (reuse the original description, no regeneration needed), [Identity 2] Standard transformed warrior (cite the original transformation key_element ID), [Identity 3] Awakened sublimation state (standard battle attire as the base, with sublimation patterns and light add-on layers spontaneously emerging); annotate the awakening trigger condition (e.g., "triggered by infusion of companions' Color Identity light after the SF-2 sacrifice") so assembly can confirm the first-appearance timeline node.
- **7.2 Sublimation Accessories (Sublimation, Not Replacement)**: wings of light/cape of light (semi-transparent wings of light spontaneously unfolding from the shoulder blades or a glowing long cape at the waist, semi-transparent prisms refracting the Color Identity interwoven with all companions' colors, a mirrored contrast to the villain's dark long cape), sublimated crown/hair ornament (the original crown gains a new light add-on layer, signature sublimation feature: a new star-shaped/moon-shaped main gem at the crown's center or tiny meteor trails in the hair, "same origin as the standard form but in fuller bloom"), weapon sublimated state (enhanced self-luminous effects or a briefly brand-new form, e.g., staff-tip crystal extension/ribbon-formed light blade, note the retracted state after use), light pattern layer (constellation patterns/magic runes echoing the Color Identity spontaneously emerging on the arms/collarbone/abdomen, annotate the pattern shape reference, e.g., "Orion chain pattern" "crescent flowing-line pattern").
- **7.3 Dedicated Color Scheme**: main color sublimation (saturation and brightness raised in sync, e.g., pink → platinum rose light, closer to pure white light than a single hue), companion color fusion layer (trace amounts of all companions' Color Identity mixed in as a light-scattering prism effect, list each blended-in companion color and its location one by one, e.g., "fire warrior's orange-red scattered at the wing edges"), hair color change (evolving toward near-pure-white or a rainbow gradient blending all members' Color Identity, note the roots→tips gradient direction and the final hue), pupil light burst (Color Identity main color + pure white light core, a dual-layer burst, uniform single pupil color forbidden, note the burst intensity: faint glimmer/moderate starburst/full-frame burst).
- **7.4 Cross-Shot Reference Constraints**: independent asset_id binding (do not reuse the standard transformation form image); first-appearance frame locking (annotate the shot ID of first reference, i.e., the SF-3c Hero Pose wide freeze frame, subsequent references must be visually consistent with the first-appearance version); form retraction after the season finale (for a one-time transcendence, note the visual treatment for returning to the standard battle attire, e.g., "wings slowly dissipating into particles/crown returning to its original form").

### Daily Life Storyboards DL-1~DL-5 (No-Combat School Warmth/Friendship Deepening/Secret Identity Subplot Episodes)
Built around "emotional accumulation" rather than "energy bursts": gentle pacing, with static shots or extremely slow push-ins. Everyday scenes must have independently created element scenes that form a warm contrast with battle/dream sequences (e.g., `[Scene_Classroom_Morning]` morning light slanting across desks, `[Scene_Rooftop_Lunch]` lunch on the rooftop, etc.), with a palette dominated by warm yellow/cream white/pale-blue sunlight. Beat types: DL-1 warm companionship (natural everyday interactions/medium or wide shots/static camera or extremely slow lateral pan/preferring no dialogue or short, light lines ≤8 characters/**Color Identity light effects or transformation elements forbidden**), DL-2 secret-identity micro-tension (identity nearly exposed/abrupt cut from wide shot to a facial close-up/expression shifting from relaxed to subtly alert/background color temperature kept at everyday warmth without triggering battle light effects/"anomaly within the ordinary"; suspense not developed, only foreshadowed), DL-3 friendship-deepening dialogue (deep exchange/two-shot medium framing or alternating close-ups/camera essentially locked/lines slightly longer, each ≤20 characters, genuine rather than declarative/soft piano or guitar as a very-low-volume accompaniment), DL-4 solitary contemplation (inner-monologue moments/medium shot or close-up/extremely slow push-in or fully locked/conveyed via micro-expressions and changes in ambient light/no dialogue needed/"unresolved question" state—unlike E-2, here it is a question, not an answer), DL-5 everyday-afterglow closure (sunset farewells/exchanging goodnights/wide shot or back-view shot/backlight or warm side light tracing silhouettes/camera slowly pulling back or holding still as the character walks away/score may link into the ED intro). Standard paragraph structure: opening establishment (1–2 shots) → warm interaction (2–4 shots, DL-1) → secret-identity micro-tension (0–2 shots, DL-2, optional) → friendship or inner deepening (1–3 shots, DL-3/DL-4) → everyday-afterglow closure (1–2 shots, DL-5). Constraints: **introducing battle grammar is forbidden** (dramatic zoom-in/low-angle heroic composition/Color Identity light-effect bursts/transformation device summons); dialogue density (DL-1/DL-5 prefer dialogue-free body-language/expression storytelling); daily→battle episode sensory switching (daily episodes close with warm yellow + stillness, battle episodes open with starry blue-purple cool tones + push-in).

### Audio Track Planning (cross-shot audio)
- **BGM**: At least one music layer spanning transformation and battle, in 90s classic orchestral-magic style or retro electronic synthesizer.
- **SFX**: Cross-shot sustained magical bell chime (magical chime), orchestral sparkle, transformation device activation sound, ribbon-swishing sound.
- **OP opening theme**: An independent audio_layer (type: music) placed at the very front of the storyboard, covering the entire opening shot sequence (about 90s); style: 90s J-Pop/idol-pop, with an orchestral starlight intro (4–8 bars) and a distinctly upbeat chorus paired with transformation-climax visuals; lyric themes center on "a girl's vow of love and protection"; for the vocal version, write complete lyrics in description (verse/chorus/bridge structure) + singing voice (narration_speaker_profile: clear girlish tone/pure high register/slight emotional vibrato); for the instrumental-only version, note in layout_instruction "no vocals, keep the synth chords and foreground strings".
- **ED ending theme**: An independent audio_layer placed at the end, covering the ending sequence (about 60–90s); slower in tempo than the OP, a gentle lyrical ballad or soft city-pop, with piano and acoustic guitar carrying the main melody; lyric themes center on bonds of friendship and everyday protection, with emotion shifting from post-battle relief to gentle anticipation; layout_instruction should note the correspondence with the ending's static/semi-dynamic visuals (characters' everyday silhouettes/fading starry sky).

## Workflow

1. **Global design & specifications**: First ask whether reference prototype materials exist (character reference images = lock the daily form + transformation outfit; prop/weapon reference images = cross-shot prop consistency; scene reference images = the color base of the scene key_element; timbre reference audio = the key_element_audio base; music style reference = OP/ED style); explain that purely text-based creation is possible without materials, though fidelity is higher with them. Use `write_file_v2` to create/update Final_Video_Spec.md to lock: aspect ratio (classic 4:3 or modern 16:9), art style (90s hand-drawn cel animation), SFX/music tonality (orchestral starlight + retro synth), output language, and the confirmed list of reference materials (noting the key_element/audio_layer each material binds to).
2. **Resource analysis** (`analyze`, if uploaded): characters/props (daily vs transformation + accessories), scenes (color tone/light source/atmosphere), timbre, music style; supplement the "reference material → key_element/audio_layer mapping table". Skip if no materials.
3. **Storyboard design & asset anchoring** (`storyboard_designer`): key_elements must explicitly specify dual forms (Daily Form / Magical Form) + daily/dream dual-layer scenes + shots containing the ritualized transformation sequence (emotion trigger → light particles spreading → rotating camera → outfit assembling layer by layer → gorgeous freeze-frame Pose) and poetic magical battles; if key materials have been provided, bind them to the corresponding key_element. ⏸ **Storyboard confirmation pause**: present the overall plot flow, transformation steps, and shot breakdown; large-scale generation may proceed only after confirmation.
4. **Key-element asset generation** (`generate_image_v3`; ImageToImage preserves dual-layer forms and cross-shot consistency). **Awakened-form asset checkpoint (for season finales)**: if the storyboard contains a Season Finale Climax beat, the "Awakened Form" key_element design-sheet generation and asset_id registration/binding **must** be completed here; verify readiness before entering stage 5, and if not ready, pause to confirm the visual plan. ⏸ **Element design confirmation pause**: after the daily and transformation reference images are generated, pause for feedback to ensure 90s aesthetic fidelity.
5. **Batch generation of video shots and audio layers** (`generate_video_v3`; MultiModalToVideo binds character/scene key_element by default, bind key_element_audio when dialogue/timbre consistency is required; `generate_audio` produces BGM/transformation SFX/long narration VO).
6. **Timeline editing & render export** (`composite_video`): classic starlight transitions + high-contrast beat alignment, then export.

## media Generation Strategy (key points)

- **Image/element assets**: `generate_image_v3` (Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image 2, 2K, excellent at reproducing text/multi-sub-image layouts/hand-drawn details); character design sheets use **horizontal layout**: on the left a refined facial portrait (large eyes/starlight pupils/hairstyle), on the right the full-body form (outfit + signature accessories), with a unified, clean monochrome dreamy gradient-pink background.
- **Video shots**: `generate_video_v3` (Seedance 2.0 / Fast, 720p default, 1080p for members); single-character shots precisely reference the corresponding key_element design sheet, bind key_element_audio when dialogue/emotional shouting is present, and may include the previous shot's reference_video; **group shots**: for wide shots, pass in all on-screen characters' key_elements at once as multi-image references and map them one-to-one with `<<<image_1>>>…`; for F-1~F-4, each shot references only the key_element of the character it focuses on (the F-3/F-4 convergence shots may reference all characters together to keep colors consistent); **generate multi-character dialogue spread across different shots, and never trigger multiple characters' speech simultaneously within one shot** to avoid timbre aliasing.
- **Sound & Music**: BGM `generate_audio`(text_to_instrumental, Suno 5, `"90s magical girl anime theme, nostalgic synthpop, orchestral sparkles, fast tempo, upbeat melody"`); dialogue voice-over `generate_audio`(text to narration, MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD / ElevenLabs v3, soaring/innocent/hot-blooded/emotionally rich girlish voice timbres).
- **Awakened Form Design Sheet** (complete before SF-3c): **ImageToImage mandatory** (Nano Banana Pro, 2K), **TextToImage prohibited** (must derive by sublimation from the standard transformation form sheet to keep the visual lineage consistent). Reference image combination (by priority): ① Primary reference (required) the battle outfit base from that character's standard transformation form design sheet; ② Sublimation feature reference (required, if available) external reference images of light wings/awakened hair color/sublimated crown; ③ Companion color reference (optional) other characters' design sheets, only to guide the prismatic scatter color range. Prompt focus: explicitly state "overlay light wings/patterns/sublimated crown onto the standard battle outfit" rather than replacing it (use overlay/emerge over/add luminous layer upon), write sublimated accessory details/exclusive color schemes into each awakened key_element field one by one, horizontal two-panel layout (left: awakened facial portrait emphasizing sublimated eye color and hair color/right: full body with light wings spread + patterns + weapon sublimation); persistent negatives `no dark Color Identity elements, no villain motifs, no standard form only (must show awakened layers), no realistic textures, no modern VFX`. After generation, immediately register an independent asset_id (not shared with the standard transformation) and bind it to the "Awakened Form key_element" slot (if not yet created, first use `storyboard_designer` to add the entry); Final_Video_Spec records the asset_id and the first referencing shot ID (SF-3c); **Generation timing** must complete before SF-3c video generation, verify readiness before Planner batch generation, pause for confirmation if not ready.
- **OP/ED Generation**: vocal version `generate_audio`(lyrics_to_song, Suno 5, **prompts must not contain well-known artist names** to avoid generation failure), OP direction `"90s Japanese magical girl anime opening theme, upbeat idol-pop, soaring chorus, orchestral intro fanfare, synthesizer lead, dreamy female vocal, energetic and emotional"`, ED direction `"90s Japanese magical girl anime ending theme, soft ballad, warm acoustic guitar and piano, gentle female vocal, bittersweet and hopeful, friendship theme, city-pop influenced"`; instrumental-only version `text_to_instrumental`(Suno 5/Mureka 8, append `"no vocals, instrumental only"`); align durations to OP ~90s, ED ~60–90s, specify section structure (intro→verse→chorus→interlude→chorus→outro); after generation, register the asset_id and bind it to the corresponding OP/ED audio_layer.
