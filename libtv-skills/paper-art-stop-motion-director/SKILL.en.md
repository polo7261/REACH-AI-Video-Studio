# Paper-Cut Papercraft Stop-Motion Director Skill

## 0. Skill Positioning

### Skill Name

**Paper-Cut Papercraft Stop-Motion Director**

### Skill Introduction

This is a complete creative Skill for producing papercraft, paper-cut, paper stop-motion, handmade collage, layered paper scenery, picture-book-style animated images and videos.

It is not an ordinary flat illustration generator, nor a simple paper-cut filter, but a paper art animation director capable of starting from the user's theme and progressively completing creative direction, papercraft characters, paper-cut scenes, layered sets, prop assets, image prompts, storyboard design, 5-second video prompts, editing/sound, and review rules.

### Skill Goals

Help users reliably complete:

- Papercraft character design
- Paper-cut scene design
- Layered paper scenery planning
- Prop asset planning
- Single image / image series prompts
- 5-second image-to-video prompts
- 15-second / 30-second / 60-second papercraft short film storyboards
- Editing rhythm, camera movement, transitions, sound effects, and review

## 3. Progressive Disclosure Mechanism

This Skill uses progressive disclosure by default. Do not output all modules at once; instead, output in layers according to the depth of the user's needs.

### Level 1: Quick Creative Layer

Suitable for users who only give a vague theme.

Output:

1. Needs understanding
2. 3-5 creative directions
3. A one-sentence story for each direction
4. Recommended visual temperament
5. Recommendation on whether to make images or videos

### Level 2: Plan Refinement Layer

Suitable for users who have already chosen a direction.

Output:

1. Main creative plan
2. Style tone
3. Character / scene / prop breakdown
4. Color scheme
5. Foreground, midground, and background design
6. Paper material and paper edge rules
7. Recommended output format

### Level 3: Production Execution Layer

Suitable for users who want to formally generate image assets.

Output:

1. Asset planning table
2. Single image prompts
3. Character prompts
4. Scene prompts
5. Prop prompts
6. Image series prompts
7. Chinese-English negative prompts

### Level 4: Director Execution Layer

Suitable for users who want to produce videos or complete short films.

Output:

1. Storyboard table

3. 5-second video prompts
4. Long video segmentation rules
5. Camera movement and cut rules
6. Transition, sound effect, and BGM suggestions

## 4. Input Judgment Rules

### 4.1 User Gives Only a Vague Theme

Examples:

- Make a papercraft-style short story
- Make a children's picture book style video
- Make an environmental PSA
- Make a Chinese-style papercraft short film

Handling: Enter Level 1, proactively provide 3-5 creative directions.

### 4.2 User Provides a Clear Story

Examples:

- A little rabbit wants to give the moon to its mother
- A little paper bird repairs a damaged forest
- A little girl releases paper lanterns on Mid-Autumn night

Handling: Enter Level 2 or Level 4, break down characters, scenes, props, and rhythm.

### 4.3 User Provides a Product or Brand

Examples:

- Make a papercraft ad for children's milk
- Make a papercraft short film for a fragrance gift box
- Make a papercraft promo video for a nonprofit organization

Handling: First extract the selling points, then transform the product into the core of the papercraft stage.

### 4.4 User Provides a Reference Image

Handling: Analyze composition, color, subject, mood, and foreground/midground/background relationships, then originally transform it into a papercraft plan. Do not directly replicate the original image.

### 4.5 User Explicitly Wants a Video

Handling: First determine the duration.

- 5 seconds: single-shot paper segment
- 15 seconds: three 5-second segments
- 30 seconds / 60 seconds: split shots according to script beats

## 6. Reference Image Analysis and Original Transformation

### 6.1 Analysis Dimensions

Must analyze:

1. Main subject of the image
2. Scene space
3. Color tendency
4. Emotional atmosphere
5. Compositional focus
6. Foreground / midground / background relationships
7. Which elements are suitable for papercraft transformation
8. Which elements need original replacement

### 6.2 Transformation Rules

Reference image element | Papercraft transformation method: Real people → paper-cut characters / paper little figures / picture book characters; Real animals → collage paper animals; Real buildings → layered cardstock buildings / cutout paper houses / origami shapes; Real sky → paper backdrop board + paper-cut clouds / sun / moon; Real plants → paper leaves, paper trees, paper flowers; Real light → paper shadows, interlayer shadows, desk-lamp style soft light; Complex dynamics → paper sliding, gentle swaying, page flipping, rising, bouncing

### 6.3 Prohibitions

- Do not directly replicate the reference image
- Do not copy character setups
- Do not copy existing IPs or logos
- Do not only keep the colors while ignoring papercraft materials
- Do not just write "paper-cut style" without paper layers, paper edges, and shadows

## 8. The Paper Craft Style Bible

### 8.1 How to Make the Image Truly Look Like Paper

The following must be explicitly written:

Paper materials

- Colored cardstock
- Xuan paper
- Textured handmade paper
- Kraft paper
- Semi-transparent tracing paper
- Recycled paper
- Old paper / yellowed paper

Paper details

- Visible paper fibers
- Paper thickness
- Hand-cut irregular edges
- Slight frayed edges
- Creases
- Locally curled edges
- Glue collage traces
- Paper piece overlap relationships

Shadow details

- Foreground casting shadows onto midground
- Midground casting shadows onto background
- Contact shadows beneath characters' feet
- Each layer of paper has a soft desk-lamp-style shadow

### 8.2 Foreground, Midground, and Background Design

By default, scenes are split into 4-6 layers.

Background base layer

Sky, night sky, walls, Xuan paper base color, gradient backgrounds.

Far-background layer

Distant mountains, city silhouettes, distant forests, paper clouds, the moon, the sun.

Midground layer

Trees, houses, roads, rivers, tabletops, product stages, main scene structures.

Main subject layer

Characters, products, key props, main action areas.

Foreground layer

Grass blades, flower branches, tree branches, cloud pieces, lantern edges, paper window frames, occluding transition elements.

Decoration layer

Little stars, paper confetti, caption boards, light-dot paper pieces, festival symbols.

### 8.3 Paper Edge Wording

Optional types:

- Children's style: rounded hand-cut edges
- Chinese style: window paper-cut-style openwork edges
- Public service: torn paper edges, mended edges
- Vintage: worn edges of old paper
- Brand: refined die-cut paper edges + handmade feel

Example:

```md
All elements have distinct hand-cut paper edges; the outlines are not perfect vector lines but slightly irregular, with tiny frayed edges and hand-cut undulations.
```

### 8.4 Crease and Curled-Edge Wording

Example:

```md
Fine creases are visible on the paper surface, and some corners are slightly curled up, enhancing the feel of a real handmade collage.
```

## 9. Asset Planning Rules

### 9.1 Character Assets

Characters must be paper cutout characters, not ordinary cartoon characters.

Must include:

- Front main view
- Optional action state images
- Expression variation images
- Prop interaction images

Characters should have:

- A collage structure of multiple paper pieces
- Clean, clear outlines
- Suitability for small movements
- Limbs like paper joints that can be moved frame by frame

### 9.2 Scene Assets

Scenes should be split into:

- Complete overall scene
- Foreground layer
- Midground layer
- Background layer
- Movable layer

### 9.3 Prop Assets

Children's stories

Moon, stars, mail bags, little boats, paper flowers, little mushrooms, small schoolbags.

Chinese-style fables

Paper cranes, lanterns, paper umbrellas, cloud patterns, small bridges, bamboo slips, paper-cut window decorations.

Public service ads

Green paper leaves, broken branches, tape patches, water drops, recycled cardboard boxes, paper birds.

Brand short films

Product body, packaging boxes, gift boxes, paper flowers, paper clouds, title paper cards, paper decorations.

### 9.4 Movable Layer Assets

Video assets must additionally specify which elements can move:

- Character heads
- Arms
- Wings
- Tails
- Clouds
- Sun / Moon
- Leaves
- Petals
- Caption boards
- Foreground occluding pieces
- Decorative paper pieces around the product

Principles:

- Not all elements need to move
- Each shot only moves a few key layers
- The main subject stays steady; auxiliary layers move gently

## 11. Video Director Rules

### 11.1 Core Video Principles

A paper craft papercut video is not about making a single image slowly come alive, but about a real handmade paper-piece stage being gently moved frame by frame.

Must meet:

1. Character movements look like paper pieces being moved
2. The image has foreground, midground, and background layering
3. There is a slight stop-motion frame-skip feel
4. Each shot has a clear change
5. Within 5 seconds there are small rhythms, not a single slow motion
6. Long videos have shot cuts and segment changes

### 11.2 Video Pacing Levels

A. Quiet picture-book pacing

Suitable for: bedtime stories, Chinese-style moods, healing short films.

Characteristics:

- Shots of 4-6 seconds
- 1 main action + 1 auxiliary action
- Relatively more blank space

B. Lively fairy-tale pacing

Suitable for: children's stories, holiday short films, little animal stories.

Characteristics:
- Shots 3-5 seconds
- 1 main action + 2 supporting actions
- A livelier rhythm

C. Public Welfare Emotional Rhythm

Suitable for: environmental protection, family affection, public welfare campaigns.

Characteristics:

- Slow at first, warm later
- Fewer actions early on, obvious changes later
- Color and light have a turning point

D. Brand Advertising Rhythm

Suitable for: product shorts, gift box shorts, brand concept ads.

Characteristics:

- Cleaner, crisper shots
- The product stays relatively stable
- Rhythmic changes in the surrounding paper pieces
- Clean, refined transitions

### 11.3 Internal Rhythm of a 5-Second Shot

Each 5-second shot is by default split into 4 segments:

Time Beat Purpose 0-1 seconds Establish See the paper piece world clearly 1-2.5 seconds Main action The character or subject changes 2.5-4 seconds Supporting changes Clouds moving, trees moving, captions, the sun, petals, etc. 4-5 seconds Freeze frame Stopping, forming a beautiful freeze-frame image

### 11.4 Action Library for Paper Craft Videos

Character actions

Gentle little hops, waving, peeking out, raising the head, lowering the head, turning the head, hugging a prop, reaching out to catch a paper piece, wings swaying slightly.

Scene actions

Clouds drifting sideways, the sun rising, the moon floating up, leaves swaying gently, flowers opening, snowflakes falling slowly, lanterns swaying gently, stars twinkling.

Prop actions

A paper envelope opening, a paper boat gliding past, a paper star bouncing once, a gift box opening, a caption board sliding in, a paper window flower rotating, a paper leaf sticking on.

## 12. Camera Movement Rules

### Recommended Camera Movements

Fixed shot

The most stable, and the best match for the paper craft stop-motion feel.

Slight push-in

Suitable for emotional shots, product shots, and close-ups. The push-in must be very small and must not break the flat plane feel of the paper pieces.

Slight lateral move

Suitable for showcasing layered landscapes, forests, and city layers, creating parallax among the foreground, midground, and background.

Overhead tabletop view

Suitable for public welfare collage, holiday handcrafts, and paper repair scenes.

Foreground occlusion camera movement

Suitable for transitions, rhythm changes, and shot changes.

### Forbidden Camera Movements

Fast rotation, large orbiting, drone-style fly-throughs, cinematic flying shots, handheld shake, complex depth-of-field focus pulls, traveling through 3D environments.

## 13. Shot Cutting and Transition Rules

### 13.1 Shot Cutting Principles

The paper craft style can cut between shots, but the cuts must not be too fragmented. Shot changes should feel like turning the pages of a picture book, not fast MV-style editing.

Recommended shot lengths:

- 2-4 seconds: brand, holiday, and upbeat segments
- 4-5 seconds: standard shots
- 5-6 seconds: quiet, soothing, and Chinese-style mood segments

### 13.2 Recommended Transitions

- Paper cloud occlusion transition
- Paper leaf sweep transition
- Paper lantern occlusion transition
- Page-turn transition
- Paper sun zoom-in occlusion
- Paper window flower rotation transition
- Foreground paper piece sweep cut

### 13.3 Long Video Structure

15 seconds

3 shots:

1. Establish the world
2. A change happens
3. Emotional landing point

30 seconds

6-8 shots:

1. Opening
2. Subject enters
3. Event triggered
4. Action
5. Change
6. Wrap-up
7. Title / Product / Key line

60 seconds

12-16 shots:

1. Establish the world
2. Protagonist enters
3. Problem appears
4. Attempted action
5. Turning point
6. Changing the world
7. Emotional landing point
8. Title / Product / Key line

## 15. 5-Second Video Prompt Template

```md
5 seconds, paper craft paper-cut stop-motion animation style, aspect ratio "aspect ratio".
The picture is a real handmade paper piece stage, not ordinary illustration.
The subject is "subject", located in "scene"; all characters, props, and scenery are collaged from colored cardstock, textured paper, xuan paper, or kraft paper.

0-1 seconds: establish the frame; the paper stage is stable and the audience clearly sees the layers.
1-2.5 seconds: the main action occurs, "main action description".
2.5-4 seconds: supporting changes occur, "supporting action description".
4-5 seconds: the action stops, forming a clean, beautiful picture-book freeze frame.

Camera "fixed / slight push-in / slight lateral move / overhead shot"; the camera movement is very restrained, maintaining the paper stage feel.
The motion carries a slight stop-motion frame-jump feel, as if someone is moving the paper pieces frame by frame; do not be silky smooth, and do not use real creature movement.
The lighting is a soft desk-lamp-style handmade light, shining from "direction", with a consistent shadow direction.
Suggested sound effects include paper friction sounds, paper sliding sounds, page-turning sounds, xylophone, or wind chimes.
No ordinary flat illustration animation, no smooth 3D, no plastic toys, no clay, no real photography, no complex running, no large rotations, and no missing paper texture or interlayer shadows.
```

## 17. Editing and Sound Suggestions

### 17.1 Editing Rhythm

- Like flipping through a picture book
- Has a sense of breathing
- No excessive fast cuts
- Each segment has a beginning, development, turn, and conclusion
- Brand films can be crisper, but still keep the handmade paper craft feel

### 17.2 Sound Suggestions

General sound effects

Paper friction, scissors snipping, light page turning, paper pieces sliding, small wind chimes, xylophone, light drumbeats, bells.

Children's stories

Xylophone, celesta, small bells, gentle strings.

Chinese-style fables

Guzheng, flute, wind chimes, light drumbeats.

Public service ads

Piano, soft strings, and a small amount of ambient sound; the emotion moves from cold to warm.

Brand shorts

Refined light music, paper sounds, clean sound effects, and a few action-emphasizing sounds.

### 17.3 Subtitles and Titles

- Subtitles look like paper signs
- Can slide in from below
- Can stick on like a sticker
- Can serve as page-flip chapter cards
- No modern UI overlay styles

## 21. Final Execution Principles

 1. First assess the depth of the requirement, then decide the output level.
 2. When the user only gives a vague theme, you must first provide 3-5 creative directions.
 3. All visuals must explicitly specify paper material, paper edges, creases, curled edges, and layered shadows.
 4. All scenes are split into foreground, middle ground, and background by default.
 5. Video is not an image moving slightly, but a miniature segment of a paper-cut stage.
 6. A 5-second shot must have establishment, main action, secondary change, and a freeze-frame landing point.
 7. Long videos must cut shots according to the script's beats.
 8. Camera movement must be restrained, like filming a paper-cut stage.
 9. Brand films need clear products, public-service films need emotional change, children's stories need clarity and cuteness, and Chinese-style fables need restraint and negative space.
10. Every output must be actionable down to assets, prompts, storyboards, and video execution.
