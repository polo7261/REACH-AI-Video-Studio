## One dreamcore look + three mood tiers (lock in the mood tier before shooting begins; consistent throughout the video)

dreamcore has only **one look** (low saturation, low contrast, lifted blacks, heavy soft glow, 35mm grain, surreal yet photorealistic); the **mood tier** tunes the pink/blue ratio and the warm/cool balance. Use the English clauses below as-is: **the "grading clause" goes into MJ scene images, and the "leading style sentence" goes as the first sentence of the Seedance prompt.**

### Sweet warm nostalgia (warm, leaning pink)

- Grading clause: `dreamcore aesthetic, surreal yet photorealistic, desaturated washed-out pastel grade leaning blush-pink and cream, warm hazy afternoon glow, soft diffused light with gentle bloom, lifted matte blacks, low contrast, fine 35mm film grain, gentle soft focus`
- Leading style sentence: `Warm dreamcore music video frame, surreal yet photorealistic, blush-pink and cream desaturated pastel palette, soft hazy afternoon glow with gentle bloom, lifted matte blacks and low contrast, fine 35mm grain, weightless dreamy slow motion.`

### Ethereal purity / weightless melancholy (cool, leaning blue)

- Grading clause: `dreamcore aesthetic, surreal yet photorealistic, desaturated washed-out pastel grade leaning powder-blue and lilac, cool misty pale silvery light, soft diffused glow, lifted matte blacks, low contrast, fine 35mm film grain, gentle soft focus`
- Leading style sentence: `Cool ethereal dreamcore music video frame, surreal yet photorealistic, powder-blue and lilac desaturated pastel palette, cool misty pale light with soft glow, lifted matte blacks and low contrast, fine 35mm grain, weightless dreamy slow motion.`

### Mildly chilly nightmare (uneasy, grey-blue)

- Grading clause: `dreamcore aesthetic, surreal yet photorealistic, muted desaturated palette leaning grey-blue, flat overcast soft light, faint emptiness and stillness, lifted matte blacks, low contrast, fine 35mm film grain, gentle soft focus` (still soft-glow and low-saturation; convey the unease through emptiness/stillness, no horror elements added)
- Leading style sentence: `Faintly uneasy dreamcore music video frame, surreal yet photorealistic, muted grey-blue desaturated palette, flat overcast soft light, a sense of empty stillness, lifted matte blacks and low contrast, fine 35mm grain, weightless slow motion.`

## Fixed seeds & image-generation specs (quick reference)

> **Global defaults**: MJ quality "adaptive", Lib Image "standard quality"; **every MJ prompt ≤ 1024 characters**; all MJ scene images uniformly use **V8.2**. Prompts are **written in English** (parameters/seed code kept as-is).

- **Styling sheet = Seedream 5.0 Pro** (**2K, no trailing flags/parameters of any kind**): generate **one 16:9, white-background styling sheet with 4 full-body looks laid out horizontally left to right — 4 clearly distinct outfits**, real-person model, face and full body clearly visible; the user **picks 1 of these 4** (the pick is locked in as the main look for the whole video). **If a styling reference image exists, use it as Seedream's image reference; portrait/face reference images are never fed to Seedream.** The prompt is natural-language English (gender/ethnicity/styling direction + outfit/character baseline constraints), **with no** `--sref` **/** `--ar` **/** `--v` **/** `--no` **trailing flags**.\
  Example: `one 16:9 2K white-background styling sheet; the same [gender] [ethnicity] real adult model shown as four different full-body looks side by side, four clearly distinct outfits, all in the chosen mood's dreamcore styling direction (sweet warm nostalgia sweet doll / ethereal purity all-white nightwear+JK / mildly chilly nightmare icy powder-blue — see "Styling Language"); for a male subject swap any skirt for trousers, keep the same fabrics and elements; clear face and full body, natural hands; modest tasteful fully-covered outfit, high modest neckline, opaque non-transparent fabrics, knee-length or longer skirt (or with matching modesty shorts); clean skin with no tattoos and no piercings; pure white seamless background, even soft studio light, photographic`
- **Three-view sheet = Lib Image** (mandatory, default **standard quality**): once the styling is confirmed, **expand from the selected full-body look into a 16:9 white-background full-body three-view sheet**. **Hard requirement: the user-selected Seedream styling sheet must be wired into Lib Image as the image input / reference connection** — without that connection, the three-view sheet will strike out on its own and fail to match the selected look, voiding the entire step; the three-view sheet's **cut / colors / silhouette / fabric / proportions (and the face, when no portrait reference exists) must match the selected styling sheet item for item**, only adding the side / back views. **Real-person model**; **mandatory layout: all content within a single image, 16:9, full body in left/center/right three views (front/side/back), no head close-ups / big-head shots**; **with a portrait reference image → fully cover the three-view face with a solid-white circular sticker + small Chinese text in the bottom-right corner 「人脸单独提供 · 此图仅用于服装造型参考」** (the real face is locked in Seedance by the original image); **without a portrait reference image → keep the face from the chosen Seedream styling** (the face is the identity).\
Key point: `using the selected styling image as image input/reference, expand it into a single 16:9 white-background turnaround; keep the exact same outfit (style / color / silhouette / fabric) and proportions as the input, only three full-body views — left / center / right (front / side / back) of the same real adult photographic model, no head close-ups, infer side and back consistently, clean skin no tattoos no piercings, pure white seamless background, even soft studio light, photographic`. **When a portrait reference is provided**: `cover each face with a plain solid white circular sticker, add a small Chinese caption at the bottom-right: 人脸单独提供 · 此图仅用于服装造型参考` (if no portrait, keep the face — no sticker, no caption).
- **Scene image = Midjourney V8.2** (default **adaptive**, prompt ≤ 1024 characters), **fixed dreamcore seed** `--sref 4658243766 --profile yp7vzjb`, **panoramic, no people, zero text, surreal yet photorealistic**.\
  Tail string: `… --sref 4658243766 --profile yp7vzjb --ar <16:9|4:3|9:16> --v 8.2 --no text, words, letters, numbers, captions, signage, logo, watermark, extra patterns, cartoon, illustration`
- **Video = Seedance 2.5**, inputs = scene image + white-background three-view turnaround (locks styling/physique) + **face reference** (portrait reference provided → the original image/video; none → the face of the selected Seedream look; **the portrait reference is not fed to the styling model — only fed here**) + **audio slices of the lipsync segments (wired to the audio input port)**.

## Workflow (pause for confirmation at each stage)

### Stage 0 · Before the shoot (one intake card; once the questions are asked, stop and wait for uploads)

**Pre-shoot intake card (every question below is an "independent question", asked one after another within the same card; never fold two questions into the options of one question, and never split across multiple cards; when a conditional question doesn't apply, let the user "leave blank/skip"). After the questions, stop and wait for the user to upload audio/reference images.**

1. **Audio (single choice)**: Do you already have a song / BGM? — `I do, I'll upload it later` / `No, generate one for me`.
2. **Song style + theme (free input; fill in only if "No" was chosen in Q1; leave blank if you have audio)**: What style and theme do you want? (dreamcore tracks go **dream pop / ambient / bedroom pop** — hazy, slow-tempo, heavy reverb)
3. **Vocals (gender + language)**: Which vocals? **Gender (male / female) + singing language** (Chinese / English / Korean / …). (If audio was uploaded, pre-fill by listening; editable)
4. **Portrait / styling reference images**: Do you have a face reference image (or a &lt;15s video) / a styling reference image? — **as long as a portrait reference exists → the protagonist's gender and ethnicity always follow the reference; leave Q5 blank**; if not, fill in Q5 as usual.
5. **Character ethnicity (free input; fill in only if Q4 was "no portrait reference")**: Recommended based on the language from Q3 (Chinese → Asian, English → Western, Korean → Asian…); editable.
6. **Subtitles**: Does the final video need subtitles? Yes → choose a language; No → no subtitles. (Subtitles are overlaid only in Stage 5; generated footage always has zero text)
7. **Mood preset**: sweet warm nostalgia / ethereal purity / slightly chilly nightmare (sets the pink/blue ratio).
8. **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 / 4:3 / 9:16?
9. **Resolution**: 480p / 720p / 1080p?

> **Conclusions**: **protagonist gender = vocal gender**; **ethnicity = portrait reference present → follow the reference (leave Q5 blank) / absent → recommend by language and confirm with the user**; **do not ask gender/ethnicity separately, and do not ask about duration either** (set by [Segment Selection]). All 9 questions live in this one card — one thing per question, each with its own clean options.

**Audio ready / music confirmation (before slicing)**:

- **Audio provided** → use the user's uploaded audio.
- **No audio** → generate a version with lyrics and vocals based on style/theme + vocals (gender/language) (**genre: dream pop / ambient / bedroom pop**) → **after generation, pause and wait for the user to confirm they're happy with the music** (can be redone/fine-tuned); slice only once satisfied. (When generating, keep the style field and the lyrics field separate — don't sing the descriptive words as lyrics; music prompt ≤ 1024 characters.)

**Two must-do steps after audio confirmation (before storyboarding and video generation)**:

- **① Audio slicing (+ get the lyrics)**: slice into **multiple consecutive clips, each ≤15s** (cut according to the corresponding video durations in seconds), and at the same time **obtain the exact lyrics — for internal lip-sync alignment / segment selection only, never written into any prompt sent to a model**; each slice of the lipsync segments will act as an **audio anchor wired in and fed to Seedance 2.5 to drive the lip movement**. **After slicing, stop and wait for the user to confirm the slices.**
- **② Segment selection**: ask "**Which segments do you want to use for this MV?**". **Recommend consecutive segments (each ≤15s)** (for smoother joins between segments); **recommend picking segments with vocals/lyrics for lipsync — purely instrumental segments get no lipsync by default** (instrumental segments get only the dreamcore performance, no singing). **Once selected, the duration is computed automatically (= sum of the segments' seconds = number of video segments); never ask "how long do you want it"**; the segment count is fixed by this step.

**Styling branching (depending on whether a portrait / styling reference was uploaded in Q4, four cases apply; in every case, first "understand the styling direction + flesh it out into a complete spec combined with the styling language below", without discarding the image or copying it verbatim. Unified flow: Seedream produces a 16:9 white-background 2K image with 4 looks in a horizontal row → the user picks 1 → Lib Image expands the selected look into the three-view turnaround)**:

> **Iron rule: portrait/face reference images are never fed to the styling model (Seedream)** (Seedream only accepts **styling reference images** as image reference); **the face reference is fed together with the three-view turnaround only at the final Seedance video-generation step** (locks the real face; **hairstyle is the user's choice: keep the reference's hairstyle / switch to the three-view turnaround's hairstyle**). **How faces in the three-view turnaround are handled depends solely on "whether a real portrait reference exists": yes → cover the face with a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese caption at the bottom-right; no → keep the face of the selected Seedream look.**

- **① Portrait reference only**: the portrait is **not fed to Seedream**; Seedream generates the four looks from the text spec (no tail string) → pick 1 → Lib Image three-view turnaround, **face fully covered by a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese caption at the bottom-right 「人脸单独提供 · 此图仅用于服装造型参考」**; the portrait is fed together with the three-view turnaround **only at the Seedance step** (locks the real face; **hairstyle chosen by the user**).
- **② Styling reference only**: the styling image **serves as the image reference for Seedream** + flesh out the spec → Seedream generates the four looks → pick 1 → Lib Image generates the three-view turnaround with **face + styling** based on the selected look, **face not masked** (the selected look's face is the identity and also serves as the Seedance face reference).
- **③ Both portrait and styling references**: **only the styling image serves as the image reference for Seedream; the portrait is not fed** + flesh out the spec → Seedream generates the four looks → pick 1 → Lib Image three-view turnaround, **face covered by a pure-white circular sticker + small Chinese caption at the bottom-right**; the portrait is fed together with the three-view turnaround **only at the Seedance step** (locks the real face; **hairstyle chosen by the user**).
- **④ Neither**: using **the user-confirmed ethnicity + gender recommended from vocal language + gender** + the fleshed-out spec, Seedream generates the four looks (no tail string) → pick 1 → Lib Image three-view turnaround, **face not masked** (the selected look's face is the identity and also serves as the Seedance face reference). **Never ask gender/ethnicity separately.**
**dreamcore styling language (align to this when drafting the spec later; written in English; one set per mood tier, clearly differentiated from one another; unisex — for male looks, replace all skirts with trousers while keeping fabrics/elements/palettes unchanged; no tattoos or piercings on skin; skirt-length floor = knee-length or longer / or paired with matching-color modesty shorts)**:

- **Sweet nostalgia (warm, leaning pink / apricot / cream)** — References: doll collar, lace tulle skirt, mary-jane doll shoes, cute hair accessories:\
  `nostalgic sweet doll styling — peter-pan / doll collar blouse, layered tulle tutu skirt (knee-length or with matching modesty shorts) or soft layered chiffon skirt, mary-jane doll shoes, striped or lace knee socks, ribbons / bows / patchwork hair accessories, soft bangs; warm pastel palette (blush-pink, apricot, cream); vintage doll-like sweetness` Male: skirt → `soft tailored pastel trousers or cropped pants`; keep the doll-collar top, ribbon/bow hair accessories (kept minimal), and mary-jane-style shoes; palette and fabrics unchanged.
- **Ethereal purity (predominantly white)** — References: nightwear elements, JK school-uniform elements, all-white layering:\
  `ethereal all-white styling — nightwear-inspired layered white blouse with lace-trim bloomers, plus JK school-uniform elements (knee-length pleated skirt, sailor / doll collar, ribbon tie), soft chiffon layers, white knee socks, delicate white hair piece; pure innocent airy vibe; monochrome white / off-white / cream palette` Male: skirt → `white JK-style shirt with ribbon tie and tailored white trousers, or white pajama-style layered shirt with lace-trim trousers`; likewise all-white, with nightwear/school-uniform elements and soft gauze fabrics.
- **Chilly nightmare (predominantly pale blue / cool tones)** — References: pale-blue button-front pinafore dress, ruffle puff sleeves, gloves, star hair accessories:\
  `cool-toned dreamcore doll styling in icy powder-blue — button-front pinafore or vest over a white ruffle puff-sleeve top, matching gloves, star / snowflake hair clips, pleated hem (knee-length or with matching modesty shorts); faintly cold pristine uneasy mood; cool pastel palette (powder-blue, ice-blue, cool grey)` Male: skirt → `icy powder-blue button-front vest over a ruffle-cuff shirt with matching tailored trousers and gloves`; likewise cool-toned pale blue, with button-front/ruffle elements.

At most one slightly brighter accent color; everything else stays within that tier's palette; observe the dress-code floor (modest full coverage, no tattoos or piercings, and skirts paired with matching-color modesty shorts). **These three styling sets correspond one-to-one with the "palette clause / lead-in style sentence" of the three mood tiers above**.

### Stage 1 · Final_Video_Spec (already folded into the pre-shoot card)

Mood tier / aspect ratio / image quality **were already asked in that pre-shoot card** (items 7\~9); no separate card is opened here. **Duration is not asked — segment count / total length is determined by [Clip Selection]** (however many ≤15s segments are selected = that many videos = total length); each segment = one ≤15s clip, assembled back-to-back in sequence. The gritty / dreamy feel comes from grain and color grading, not from low resolution.

> The spec is confirmed together with the card; no separate pause needed.

### Stage 2 · Storyboard

- **Styling image**: depending on which of the four cases applies, use **Seedream 5.0 Pro to produce one 16:9 white-background 2K image with 4 different full-body looks in a horizontal row** (**no tail string**; only styling reference images may be fed as image references — no portrait images) → the user **picks 1 of the 4** → **pause to confirm** → use **Lib Image to expand the chosen look into a 16:9 white-background three-view sheet** (**hard requirement: the selected Seedream styling image must be wired into Lib Image as an image input / reference connection** — without the connection the three-view sheet drifts, mismatches the chosen look, and the whole step is void; the three-view sheet's cut / colors / silhouette / fabric / proportions must match the chosen styling image point by point; a real human model; **within one image, full-body left/center/right three views, no enlarged head**; **if a portrait reference image exists → cover the face with a plain white circular sticker + a small Chinese-text annotation in the bottom-right corner; if not → keep the face**) → **pause again to confirm**. **Styling observes the dress-code floor** (modest full coverage, no large sheer areas, high neckline, skirt knee-length or longer, no tattoos or piercings). **Costume and styling stay locked for the whole video; "Look 1 / Look 2" are only produced again if the user explicitly requests a costume change**.

- **Scenes** (immediately after the three-view sheet is confirmed): based on the number of selected segments (= number of locations), **first give 3–4 open-ended scene options (self-description also allowed) → as soon as the user has picked which scenes to use, generate the images immediately per Stage 3**; for self-described scenes, follow the user's intent + apply the mood-tier palette + zero text + panoramic with no people + surreal yet photorealistic. Multiple shots at the same location share that one selected image.

- **Storyboard script (must be produced and confirmed before video generation; use a table)**: list segment by segment, shot by shot, with fixed columns **Segment/Shot no. | Audio slice (segment-level) | Visual (character performance + scene + crack) | Duration (s) | Camera (handheld / slow-mo) | Performance/expression | Link to adjacent segments**. **For each segment, an "audio slice" column immediately follows the first column, clearly stating which slice the segment uses**\*\*(slice number) + **with vocals (→ lip-sync) / instrumental (→ no lip-sync)** — when generating the video, wire that slice into Seedance accordingly; writing the audio into the table significantly lowers the chance of "audio never wired in, lip-sync/BGM lost entirely". Example:

  | Seg/Shot | Audio slice | Visual | Duration (s) | Camera | Performance/expression | Link to adjacent segments |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1-1 | Slice ① (vocals → lip-sync) | protagonist already on the water surface amid the pools, close-up, singing along | 4 | slow push-in | eyes slowly opening, light breathing, lips matching the lyrics | person in frame from the opening, no empty shot |
  | 1-2 | ↑ same segment, Slice ① | continuous camera move rising, medium shot | 4 | slow-mo | hair drifting, lost in thought | continuous camera move in the previous shot's direction |
  | 2-1 | Slice ② (instrumental → no lip-sync) | walking into a sea of flowers, medium shot | 4 | slow pan | not singing, gently twirling | carries over from Seg 1's final shot: subject position / camera direction continue |

  **≥4 shots per 15s**, with slow-mo landing points marked; **no empty shot for the first shot — the character is in frame from the very start**; **with multiple segments, every adjacent pair (last shot of the earlier ↔ first shot of the next) must have a logical / filmic-language link** (action carryover, compositional echo, camera-move direction, subject position / mood handoff) so that even clean cuts between segments flow; **no cross-dissolves anywhere in the piece**. **Each segment's "audio slice" is the basis for the Stage 4 wiring — connect the corresponding slice to Seedance's audio input per the table (hard requirement).**

- **Music (already in place from Stage 0: binding/generation + slicing + clip selection)**: here, mark transition / slow-mo landing points to the beats of **each selected slice**; and mark **which segments do lip-sync (with vocals) and which instrumental segments don't**.

> Pause to confirm (styling image + three-view sheet + scene images + storyboard script + (if no audio yet) music).

### Stage 3 · MJ V8.2 Scene Images (executed immediately after the three-view sheet is confirmed)

Describe the chosen panoramic empty scene (space type + layout + key objects + **one surreal crack** + composition); **surreal yet photo-real, no people, zero text/logos/patterns**; apply the selected mood tier's palette clause; append the scene-seed tail string (see quick reference). **As soon as the user has picked which scenes to use, generate the images immediately**: 1 set of 4 panoramas per location, **multiple sets can be generated at once in parallel** — no need to wait serially for each one; after generation, **each location independently picks 1 of its 4**. Multiple shots at the same location share that single chosen image.
> **Every location must be confirmed via a pick-one-of-four** (the four options may be presented together but selected individually); only proceed to the next stage once all scenes are confirmed.

### Stage 4 · Seedance 2.5 Video (one clip per segment; duration = that segment's audio rounded up, then trimmed; generated one clip at a time)

Prompt structure (in order, written in English):

1. **Leading style sentence** (pick sweet-warm / ethereal / chilly per the mood tier, consistent across the whole film).
2. **Character assembly + person in the first frame**: three-view sheet locks the styling/body shape + face reference (**portrait reference image present → lock the real face from the original image/video; ask the user about hairstyle first: keep the reference image's hairstyle / switch to the three-view sheet's hairstyle, and write their choice into the prompt. No portrait → base it primarily on the three-view sheet, taking face and hairstyle from it**). State explicitly: "the face comes from the face reference, the styling from the three-view sheet, the hairstyle per the user's choice, placed into the scene image, keeping the real appearance." **Treat the scene image as a "space/style reference" — do not treat it as a mandatory empty-room first frame; the character is in frame from the very first shot** — the prompt explicitly states `the character is already present in the very first frame, open on the subject (medium/close), do NOT begin with an empty room establishing shot`, to avoid an opening empty shot. **Compliance**: modest and fully covered, no large sheer areas, high neckline, skirt not too short, no tattoos or piercings on the skin; **if wearing a skirt, write** `wearing matching-tone modesty shorts underneath, fully covered and modest`. **Outfit and styling must exactly match the three-view sheet and remain unchanged throughout the entire film.**
3. **Lip-sync + audio wiring (segments with vocals)**: **follow the "audio slice" column for that segment in the storyboard table and wire the corresponding slice node into Seedance 2.5's audio input** — mandatory; skip the wiring and you get neither the lip-sync drive nor the BGM and the whole segment is wasted (the table already states which slice to use and whether to lip-sync; just follow it). Wire in that segment's **≤15s audio slice as the audio anchor**, with the character's **mouth/jaw exactly matching the incoming audio** (`lips and jaw precisely synced to the incoming audio, natural gentle singing expression, dreamy and soft`); **never write real lyrics into the prompt**; **for purely instrumental segments the character does not sing, but that segment's slice is still wired in and kept as BGM** (`no singing, dreamy performance only` — do not write anything like "no music / no BGM").
4. **Storyboard**: `Shot1 …(camera continues / slowly moves to)… Shot2 …`, one continuous clip (duration = that segment's audio rounded up), **≥4 shots**; shots are joined via **continuous camera movement / slow drifting motion** or a few **clean hard cuts**; **never write dissolve / cross-dissolve / fade in-out / morph** (AI-generated dissolves usually come out blurry and muddy). Each shot = shot size + movement (slow handheld / overcranked) + performance.
5. **Movement**: `slow dreamy camera, natural handheld with subtle breathing motion`; for overcranked shots `slow motion / overcranked, weightless floating`; a single continuous take only when needed.
6. **Audio + duration + zero text**: the generated footage must have **zero text / no logos / no burned-in subtitles at all times** (subtitles, if needed, are overlaid in Stage 5 in the chosen language). **Each segment relies on the wired-in audio slice to drive the lip-sync + serve as the BGM**; **the prompt must never contain** `no music` **/** `no audio` **/** `no sound` **/** `no BGM` **/** `无音乐` **or similar wording** (it will suppress the lip-sync and the BGM); incoming music is always kept as BGM, the original audio is left unaltered, and **no real lyrics are written**; for instrumental segments just write "the character does not sing, only performs." **Duration = that segment's audio rounded up to a whole second**; Stage 5 then trims to the audio's actual length. Negative prompt `text, words, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, extra patterns, cartoon, illustration, stiff, rigid, frozen, robotic motion, dissolve, crossfade, fade transition, morph, tattoo, piercing` (compliance is achieved with **positive wording**; the negative prompt must **not** include any NSFW/exposure-related sensitive words, and must **not** include `no music`/`no BGM`).

**Default performance** (when the user hasn't specified, inject the dreamcore defaults; every shot gets ≥1 expression + 1 action, slow/gentle): `serene wistful presence, slow ethereal movement, gentle gaze drifting, weightless dreamlike detachment, soft turning of the head, dress and hair drifting`.\
**Universal anti-stiffness (add to every shot)**: `subtle breathing with chest gently rising and falling, hair and fabric micro-movement, expression slowly shifting (e.g. serene → faintly wistful), a slow soft blink, relaxed natural posture, natural unhurried lifelike movement easing in and out` (when lip-syncing: `jaw and lips actually moving to the incoming audio`).

> Generate one clip at a time and confirm each one individually.

### Stage 5 · Assembly (video_assembler)

When joining multiple segments in sequence, **use a clean cut at every junction between two segments, but let logic / shot language carry the transition** (matching action / composition / camera-move direction / subject position / emotion — see the storyboard) so the cut flows smoothly; **no dissolves / fades / cross-fades anywhere in the film** (dreamcore included — no flash cuts, no flashing large text); **subtitles/titles are overlaid only, never burned into the generated footage**; logo PNGs may be overlaid; **each segment is trimmed to its audio's actual duration** (rounded up at generation, trimmed back here). **Music: do not treat the original music as a separate track to be spliced back in — directly reuse the music already embedded in each generated segment** (the audio fed in at generation and preserved as-is); joining the segments together with their embedded audio tracks forms a continuous soundtrack, with lip-sync perfectly matched to the music and the whole film continuous. **Subtitles (only when "needed" was selected on the card)**: overlay the subtitles **on top of** the finished film in the chosen language (overlay only, never burned into the picture). Guide the export.

## Reference Data

### Tempo → shots per 15s (dreamcore version, hard floor of ≥4, transitions mainly via continuous camera movement)

| Mood / Tempo | Shots | Transitions & techniques |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ultra-slow ethereal | 4 | Mainly a flowing single-take continuous camera move, almost no cuts; long takes + overcranked, relying on micro-movement and performance so the shot never stalls |
| Slow lyrical | 4–5 | Mainly continuous camera movement linking shots, occasional clean hard cut; overcranked shots with a clear landing point |
| Mid-tempo groove | 5–6 | Continuous camera movement + a few clean on-beat cuts; alternate long and short shots |

> Whatever the shot count, it is all generated in one pass within a single ≤15s clip; **shots are linked by continuous camera movement, and no dissolve/fade appears anywhere in the film**; between segments use clean carry-over cuts — don't rely on jarring hard cuts throughout.

### Scene library (pick a leaning for each mood tier; all are wide, people-free shots, each containing one surreal rift — surreal yet realistic)

- **Sweet-warm nostalgia**: nostalgic girl's bedroom · indoor pool room · sea of flowers / meadow · old shopping-mall atrium · sunlit living room. Rifts: the water reflecting clouds that aren't there, floating bubbles, windows overexposed into a glow.
- **Ethereal purity / weightless melancholy**: cloud/sky bedroom · endless white corridor · underwater room · mirrored endless space · gauze-draped room. Rifts: weightless floating, corridors extending endlessly, the boundary between water and air vanishing.
- **Chilly nightmare**: empty classroom · old-CRT living room · mirror house · empty field in fog · motionless playground. Rifts: still objects that move, stopped old clocks, mirror reflections half a beat behind, stillness in the fog.

> "Surreal rift" = anomalous reflections / floating objects / endless extension / still objects that move / overexposed light blooms, etc. (surreal yet realistic — not gory, not horror). If a longer film runs short on locations, revisit the same location (with different lighting / rift).
